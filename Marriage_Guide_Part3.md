## Introduction — The Timing Challenge

> *"Vivāha-kālaḥ saptameśena, śukrena, kārakenāpi;  
> Daśā-gocarābhyāṃ nirṇeyaḥ — samāgama-tri-vargāt."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.78, Vivāha Kāla Adhyāya**  
> *(Marriage timing is determined from the 7th lord, Venus, the natural karaka;  
> and confirmed through the triple agreement of Dasha, Gochara, and Navamsha)*

The **triple agreement** principle is the foundation of marriage timing:
```
  AGREEMENT 1: Dasha period (Mahadasha or Antardasha)
               must involve 7th lord, Venus, or Darakaraka
  AGREEMENT 2: Transit (Gochara) of Jupiter and/or Saturn
               must activate H7, H7 lord, or Venus by position/aspect
  AGREEMENT 3: Navamsha activation
               D9 Lagna lord or D9 7th lord's period should be active

  Event = CONFIRMED when 2 of 3 agree; CERTAIN when all 3 align.
```

## STEP 9 — Dasha Periods for Marriage

> *"Saptameśa-daśāyām, śukra-daśāyām, dāra-kāraka-daśāyām;  
> Vivāhaḥ sambhavati — bhāva-balādisūdhe sati."*  
> — **Phaladeepika Ch.7, Vivāha-Yoga Adhyāya**  
> *(Marriage happens in the dasha of 7th lord, Venus, or Darakaraka —  
> when the house strength and other conditions are satisfied)*

### 9.1 Primary Dasha Triggers for Marriage

```
MOST LIKELY MARRIAGE DASHAS (in order of probability):

  RANK 1: 7th Lord Mahadasha or Antardasha
    → H7 lord directly activates marriage house themes
    → Most reliable single trigger

  RANK 2: Venus Mahadasha or Antardasha (♂)
           Jupiter Mahadasha or Antardasha (♀)
    → Natural karaka period; attraction and partnership themes peak

  RANK 3: Darakaraka Mahadasha or Antardasha
    → The soul-karaka for spouse is activated

  RANK 4: Navamsha Lagna lord Dasha
    → The D9 chart's first house lord activating = marriage karma activated

  RANK 5: Planets occupying H7 in the natal chart
    → Any planet in H7 (if present) — its Dasha can trigger marriage

  RANK 6: 2nd Lord (Dhana; family formation) Dasha
    → Secondary indicator; often accompanies marriage

  RANK 7: Lagna Lord Dasha
    → Sometimes triggers marriage when the native is “ready”
```

### 9.2 The 4-Layer Dasha Verification

```
For each Dasha period under consideration:

  CHECK A: What does the MD lord own? Does it include H7, H2, or H11?
  CHECK B: What does the AD lord own? Does it complement the MD lord?
  CHECK C: Are MD and AD lords related? (Mutual aspect, conjunction,
           or one is placed in the other's sign = stronger activation)
  CHECK D: Is the period free from major affliction?
           (Combust, debilitated, or aspected by severe malefic
           without mitigation = marriage delayed or difficult)
```

## STEP 10 — Transit (Gochara) Triggers for Marriage

> *"Gocara-gurau saptame — vivāha-yoga-dāyakaḥ;  
> Gocara-śanau saptame vā saptameśe vā saptama-śuddhāv api."*  
> — **Sarvartha Chintamani Ch.10** + **UK Kanda 5**  
> *(Jupiter transiting H7 = gives marriage yoga; Saturn transiting H7 or  
> conjuncting H7 lord or Venus = also triggers marriage)*

### 10.1 Jupiter Transits (Guru Peyarchi) for Marriage

```
MOST POWERFUL JUPITER TRANSIT POSITIONS for marriage:
  FROM NATAL MOON (primary reference):
    H7  = most direct marriage trigger
    H5  = romance, engagement, courtship
    H2  = family formation; marriage settlement
    H11 = gains through marriage; relationship enters life

  FROM NATAL LAGNA (secondary reference):
    H7  = activates natal H7 themes directly
    H5  = romance/love materializes

  FROM H7 CUSP:
    Jupiter conjunct natal H7 lord = direct activation

"DOUBLE JUPITER" trigger (most powerful):
  Jupiter transiting H7 from BOTH Moon AND Lagna simultaneously
  OR Jupiter transiting H7 from Moon while also aspecting natal Venus
  → Near-certain marriage event in that transit year
```

### 10.2 Saturn Transit for Marriage

```
Saturn's role in marriage timing is often overlooked:

  MARRIAGE-TRIGGERING Saturn positions:
    Saturn transiting H7 from Lagna = activates the 7th house
    Saturn transiting on natal Venus = Venus themes crystallize
    Saturn transiting 7th lord's natal position = lord activated

  NOTE: Saturn transit to H7 can also delay or test marriage
    → Whether it grants or delays depends on Saturn's NATAL dignity
    → Saturn = Yoga Karaka for some lagnas (Taurus, Libra)
    → In that case, Saturn's H7 transit GRANTS marriage
    → For other lagnas, Saturn H7 transit may delay 1-2 years
    → The Dasha must also support for marriage to happen during Saturn transit

Saturn exception rule:
  > *"Śanau saptame gate — vivāhaḥ dīrghāyaasena;  
  >  Guror-drṣṭi-sahaite — vivāho bhavati dhruvam."*
  > — UK Kanda 5
  (Saturn in H7 alone = delayed marriage; WITH Jupiter's aspect = certain)
```

### 10.3 Rahu/Ketu Transit for Marriage

```
Rahu/Ketu axis transiting through:
  H7/H1 axis: Major relationship karma activated; often foreign or
               unconventional marriage; past-life partner meeting
  H1/H7 axis: Same — major identity shifts through relationship
  H5/H11 axis: Romance, attraction, social connections

Rahu in H7 from Moon: Meeting an unusual, magnetic partner;
                       cross-cultural or age-gap relationship possible
Ketu in H7 from Moon: Past-life connection surfaces; spiritual partnership;
                       may also indicate separation from existing relationship
```

## STEP 12 — Special Yogas for Marriage Timing

### 12.1 Vivaha Yoga — Classical Marriage Combinations

> *"Saptameśe bale, śukrena drṣṭe, lagnam śubbha-grāse;  
> Vivāha-yoga-pūrṇasya — kāla-sauhardyam avasyam."*  
> — **Jataka Parijata Ch.7** *(When H7 lord is strong, Venus aspects, and Lagna is blessed:  
> a full Vivaha Yoga is formed; timing still requires Dasha-Transit support)*

```
CLASSICAL VIVAHA YOGA COMBINATIONS:

  YOGA A: H7 lord in Kendra or Trikona + Venus aspecting H7
    → Early and happy marriage

  YOGA B: Venus in H7 or H5 with no malefic aspect
    → Love marriage; beautiful, devoted partner

  YOGA C: H7 lord and Venus in mutual aspect or conjunction
    → Very auspicious; partner meets through the planet's domain

  YOGA D: Benefic planet in H7 aspected by Jupiter
    → Excellent marriage; spouse brings wisdom and prosperity

  YOGA E: Moon in H7 with waxing phase
    → Emotional, caring spouse; marriage through family circles

  YOGA F: Jupiter in H7 or aspecting H7 + H7 lord in Kendra
    → Dhana Vivaha Yoga — marriage brings great fortune
```

### 12.2 Delay Yogas — When Marriage Is Late

```
MARRIAGE DELAY INDICATORS:

  DELAY A: Saturn in H7 without benefic aspect
    Effect: Marriage after 30; partner is serious, older, or disciplined
    Source: BPHS Ch.78

  DELAY B: H7 lord in Dusthana (H6/H8/H12) without compensation
    Effect: Obstacles in finding partner; delays, separations
    Mitigation: Jupiter aspecting H7 lord

  DELAY C: Venus afflicted by Saturn or Rahu (male chart)
    Effect: Delayed or unconventional marriage; partner encountered late

  DELAY D: H7 lord debilitated without Neechabhanga
    Effect: Marital quality poor or marriage delayed

  DELAY E: Multiple malefics in H7 (especially Sun + Saturn)
    Effect: Severe delay; may indicate two marriages

  DELAY F: Ketu in H7
    Effect: Past-life karma; spiritual partner; may arrive unexpectedly
```

### 12.3 Multiple Marriage Yogas

```
CLASSICAL INDICATORS OF MORE THAN ONE MARRIAGE:

  • H7 contains both malefic and benefic planets (conflict)
  • H7 lord aspected by both Jupiter AND Saturn (pull in two directions)
  • Two strong planets in H7 (2 distinct partner energies)
  • Venus in dual signs (Gemini, Sagittarius, Pisces) with malefic aspect
  • H7 lord in dual sign + H7 aspected by Mercury or Venus in dual sign
  • The 4th from H7 (= H10) has two planets — two domestic setups

  NOTE: Multiple marriage yogas don't always manifest as literal
  marriages. They may indicate multiple significant relationships,
  a spouse with a complex background, or marriage after remarriage.
```

## STEP 13 — Muhurta — The Auspicious Marriage Moment

> *"Vivāha-muhúrte guru-śukra-bale — śubha-kālaḥ sarva-doṣa-hīnam."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Vivāha Muhúrta Prakarana**  
> *(At the marriage muhurta, the strength of Jupiter and Venus determines  
> the auspiciousness — overriding all other flaws)*

### 13.1 Essential Muhurta Requirements

```
FOR AN AUSPICIOUS MARRIAGE MUHURTA, CHECK:

  MANDATORY:
  1. Jupiter and Venus must NOT be combust (close to Sun) or retrograde
     Retrograde Jupiter/Venus = no marriage muhurta (critical rule)
     Source: MC, Vivaha Muhurta section

  2. Moon must be waxing (Shukla Paksha) or at least past full moon
     Moon in H8 from Lagna = avoid for marriage
     Source: MC, Chandra Bala

  3. Avoid Amavasya (new moon), Chaturdashi (14th lunar day)
     and Purnima (full moon) for marriage (too extreme)

  4. Lagna for muhurta: H7 must be free of malefics
     Jupiter or Venus in the muhurta H7 = auspicious

  5. Nakshatra of marriage day: Auspicious nakshatras:
     Rohini, Mrigashira, Magha, U.Phalguni, Hasta, Swati,
     Anuradha, Mula, U.Ashadha, U.Bhadra, Revati

  PROHIBITED PERIODS:
  • Guru Bala Rahita (Jupiter retrograde or combust)
  • Shukra Bala Rahita (Venus retrograde or combust)
  • Adhika Masa (intercalary month)
  • Kshaya Masa (short month)
  • Eclipse months (2 weeks before/after)
  • Ashtami, Chaturdashi, Purnima, Amavasya (tithis)
  • Rikta tithi days (4th, 9th, 14th of each paksha)

GOLDEN WINDOWS (Jupiter + Venus both direct and strong):
  These occur roughly 3-4 months per year.
  Check Panchanga (almanac) for Guru-Shukra-Bali periods.
```
