## Introduction to Ashtakuta

> *"Aṣṭakuṭāni parīkṣhet vivāha-kāle su-buddhi-mān;  
> Varṇa-vaśyādi-aṣṭakam guṇa-śodhanam uttamam."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Vivaha Prakarana, Sloka 1**  
> *(An intelligent person should examine the eight kutas at marriage time;  
> Varna, Vasya and the eight-fold testing is the highest compatibility method)*

Ashtakuta (“eight groups”) scores compatibility using the **Moon Nakshatra** of both partners. The Moon represents the mind, emotions, and instinctive nature — the most crucial factor for day-to-day marital happiness.

```
INPUTS REQUIRED:
  Groom Moon Nakshatra + Pada
  Bride Moon Nakshatra + Pada
  Groom Moon Rashi (sign)
  Bride Moon Rashi (sign)

Ajay Kumar (♂): Moon in Aries, Nakshatra = KRITTIKA Pada 1
Sravani    (♀): Moon in Sagittarius, Nakshatra = UTTARA ASHADHA Pada 1
```

## KUTA 1 — Varna (Spiritual Compatibility) — Max 1 Point

> *"Varṇe me yoga-saṃjñakam; eka-varṇa-yute dampaty-oḥ sukham."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Varna Kuta Adhyaya**

```
Varna Hierarchy (descending):
  Brahmin  > Kshatriya > Vaishya > Shudra > Mleccha

SCORING:
  Groom Varna ≥ Bride Varna = 1 point
  Bride Varna >  Groom Varna = 0 points
  (Equal varna = 1 point; groom one level below bride = 0)

RATIONALE: In traditional matching, the groom should be of equal
  or higher spiritual varna. This reflects dharmic leadership
  responsibility, NOT social hierarchy in the modern sense.

EXAMPLE — Ajay & Sravani:
  Ajay (Krittika)    = Kshatriya
  Sravani (U.Ashadha) = Kshatriya
  Equal Varna → SCORE = 1/1 ✅
```

## KUTA 2 — Vasya (Attraction & Mutual Control) — Max 2 Points

> *"Vaśye sarve pūrṇa-guṇaḥ — parasparasya vaśyatā, sneha-bandhanam."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Vasya Kuta**

```
Vasya Groups (5 animal kingdoms):
  Chatushpada (4-footed) : Aries, Taurus, Leo, 2nd half of Sagittarius, Capricorn
  Jalcharā (Water)      : Cancer, Pisces, 1st half of Capricorn
  Keetā (Insects)       : Scorpio
  Dvipada (2-footed)    : Gemini, Virgo, Libra, Aquarius, 1st half of Sagittarius
  Vanachāra (Forest)    : Leo (some schools)

SCORING:
  Same Vasya group         = 2 points
  Mutually vasya (control) = 2 points (e.g., Leo controls Aries)
  One-way vasya            = 1 point
  No vasya relationship    = 0 points

Vasya Control Table:
  Aries controls  : Scorpio, Capricorn
  Taurus controls : Cancer, Libra
  Cancer controls : Scorpio, Sagittarius
  Leo controls    : Cancer, Libra
  Virgo controls  : Pisces, Gemini
  Libra controls  : Capricorn, Virgo
  Scorpio controls: Cancer
  Sagittarius controls: Pisces
  Capricorn controls: Aries

EXAMPLE — Ajay & Sravani:
  Ajay Moon: Aries (Chatushpada)
  Sravani Moon: Sagittarius (1st half = Dvipada; 2nd half = Chatushpada)
  U.Ashadha is in Capricorn — Sravani Moon nakshatra starts in Sagittarius
  but Pada 1 of U.Ashadha falls in Capricorn navamsha → boundary case
  Traditional assignment: Sagittarius = Chatushpada (fire signs)
  Both Chatushpada → SCORE = 2/2 ✅
```

## KUTA 3 — Tara (Birth Star Compatibility) — Max 3 Points

> *"Janma-tārā-paraspare śubham — parasparasya saukhya-datvam."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Tara Kuta**

```
FORMULA:
  Count from Groom's nakshatra to Bride's nakshatra (inclusive)
  Divide by 9. Take the REMAINDER.
  Remainder maps to a Tara type:

  1 = Janma   (birth)         ✔️ AUSPICIOUS
  2 = Sampat  (prosperity)    ✔️ AUSPICIOUS
  3 = Vipat   (danger)        ❌ INAUSPICIOUS
  4 = Kshema  (well-being)    ✔️ AUSPICIOUS
  5 = Pratyak (obstacle)      ❌ INAUSPICIOUS
  6 = Sadhana (achievement)   ✔️ AUSPICIOUS
  7 = Naidhana (death/end)    ❌ INAUSPICIOUS
  8 = Mitra   (friend)        ✔️ AUSPICIOUS
  9 = Parama Mitra (best)     ✔️ AUSPICIOUS
  0 = same as 9 (Parama Mitra) ✔️

SCORING:
  Both directions auspicious = 3 points
  One direction auspicious   = 1.5 points
  Both inauspicious          = 0 points

EXAMPLE — Ajay & Sravani:
  Groom → Bride: Krittika (3) to U.Ashadha (21)
    Count: 21 - 3 + 1 = 19
    19 mod 9 = 1 (remainder) = JANMA TARA ✔️

  Bride → Groom: U.Ashadha (21) to Krittika (3)
    Count: 3 - 21 + 27 + 1 = 10
    10 mod 9 = 1 (remainder) = JANMA TARA ✔️

  Both directions = Janma Tara → SCORE = 3/3 ✅
  (Both Janma Tara in both directions = PERFECT Tara — deep karmic bond)
```

## KUTA 5 — Graha Maitri (Planetary Friendship) — Max 5 Points

> *"Rāśy-adhipau mitraḥ cet — sarva-doṣa-hīnam vivāham;  
> Maitri-pūrṇam dam-patyam — cira-sthāyi, sneha-sambandhitam."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Graha Maitri Kuta** and **Vivaha Vrindavana**  
> *(When the lords of both Moon signs are mutual friends — the marriage is free of all defects;  
> the couple enjoys lasting love and deep affection)*

```
FORMULA:
  Identify the lord of Groom's Moon sign
  Identify the lord of Bride's Moon sign
  Check their naisargika (natural) relationship using BPHS Ch.3 table

NAISARGIKA MAITRI TABLE (BPHS Ch.3):
            Sun  Moon  Mars  Mer   Jup   Ven   Sat
  Sun       —    F     F     E     F     E     E
  Moon      F    —     N     F     F     F     N
  Mars      F    N     —     E     F     N     N
  Mercury   E    N     N     —     N     F     N
  Jupiter   F    F     F     N     —     E     N
  Venus     N    N     N     F     E     —     F
  Saturn    E    N     N     N     N     F     —
  F = Friend, N = Neutral, E = Enemy

SCORING:
  Mutual Friends (F↔F)         = 5 points
  One Friend + One Neutral     = 4 points
  Both Neutral (N↔N)           = 3 points
  One Friend + One Enemy       = 1 point
  One Neutral + One Enemy      = 0.5 points
  Mutual Enemies (E↔E)         = 0 points

IMPORTANCE: This is the SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT kuta.
  Full Graha Maitri can override many other kuta deficiencies.
  Half or full score is the most reliable indicator of daily harmony.

EXAMPLE — Ajay & Sravani:
  Ajay Moon = Aries → lord = MARS
  Sravani Moon = Sagittarius → lord = JUPITER
  Mars ↔ Jupiter: Mars sees Jupiter as Friend; Jupiter sees Mars as Friend
  MUTUAL FRIENDS → SCORE = 5/5 ⭐ PERFECT

> *"Kuja-guru-śuddha-citte dam-patyam — dharma-sāhity-aikam;  
>  Yudha-sāhasaḥ guru-jñāne — saṃghaḥ sarvārthasiddhi-kārakaḥ."*
  (Mars-Jupiter combined: warrior's courage with teacher's wisdom =
  the couple achieves all aims together)

> Manuṣya-Rākṣasa-yute — madhyamam; Deva-Rākṣasa-yute — klesha-kārakam."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Gana Kuta Adhyaya**

```
GANA CLASSIFICATION:
  Deva Gana (Divine): Ashwini, Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Pushya, Hasta,
                      Swati, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati
  Manushya (Human):   Bharani, Rohini, Ardra, P.Phalguni, U.Phalguni,
                      P.Ashadha, U.Ashadha, P.Bhadra, U.Bhadra
  Rakshasa (Intense): Krittika, Ashlesha, Magha, Chitra, Vishakha,
                      Jyeshtha, Mula, Dhanishtha, Shatabhisha

GANA COMPATIBILITY SCORES:
  Deva    + Deva      = 6 points (ideal — both gentle, dharmic)
  Manushya + Manushya  = 6 points (ideal — both practical, balanced)
  Rakshasa + Rakshasa  = 6 points (ideal — both intense, direct)
  Deva    + Manushya   = 5 points (good with some adaptation)
  Manushya + Deva      = 5 points (good)
  Manushya + Rakshasa  = 1 point  (adjustment needed)
  Rakshasa + Manushya  = 1 point  (adjustment needed)
  Deva    + Rakshasa   = 0 points (clash of worldviews)

  Ajay (Krittika)    = RAKSHASA gana

  > *"Graha-maitri-pūrṇe gana-doṣo na bhādhate."*
  > — **Jyotish Ratnakar** *(When Graha Maitri is full, Gana Dosha does not afflict)*
  Since Graha Maitri = 5/5, Gana mismatch is significantly mitigated.

## KUTA 7 — Bhakut (Moon Sign Relationship) — Max 7 Points

> *"Pañcama-navama-rāśyoh — bhakūṭ-doṣa-saṃjñakam;  
> Dvitīya-dvādaśa-rāśyoh ca, ṣaṭtama-aṣṭama-rāśyoh ca."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Bhakut Kuta Adhyaya**

```
BHAKUT FORMULA:
  Count from Groom's Moon sign to Bride's Moon sign (inclusive)
  Count from Bride's Moon sign to Groom's Moon sign (inclusive)
  Identify the axis:

AUSPICIOUS axes (both directions):
  1/1 (same sign)  = 7 points
  1/7 (opposition) = 7 points (if lords are friends)
  3/11 axis        = 5 points
  4/10 axis        = 5 points
  5/9 axis         = 0 points (DOSHA) but CANCELLABLE
  2/12 axis        = 0 points (DOSHA) but CANCELLABLE
  6/8 axis         = 0 points (DOSHA — hardest to cancel)

BHAKUT DOSHA TABLE:
  2/12 (Dhan-Vyaya):  Financial drain on each other; separation risk
  5/9  (Putra-Dharma): Progeny concerns; value differences (cancellable)
  6/8  (Shatru-Ayu):  Health suffering; enmity; hardest dosha

EXAMPLE — Ajay & Sravani:
  Ajay Moon = Aries (#1)
  Sravani Moon = Sagittarius (#9)
  Aries to Sagittarius: 9 → 9th position
  Sagittarius to Aries: 4 positions
  This is the 9/4 counting → BUT the axis is 5/9 (from each other's view):
    Sagittarius is the 9th from Aries
    Aries is the 5th from Sagittarius
    → 5/9 AXIS → BHAKUT DOSHA (raw score = 0)

BHAKUT DOSHA CANCELLATION:
  > *"Pañcama-navame rāśy-adhipau mitraḥ cet —  
  >  Bhakūṭ-doṣo na vidyate; śubha-vivāham bhavati."*
  > — **MC, Bhakut Dosha Bhanga** + **JR Ch.7**
  Aries lord = Mars; Sagittarius lord = Jupiter
  Mars ↔ Jupiter = MUTUAL FRIENDS → BHAKUT DOSHA CANCELLED ✅
  Raw score: 0 → Effective score after cancellation: 7/7

2/12 Bhakut Cancellation Conditions:
  Same rule applies: if lords are mutual friends = cancelled

6/8 Bhakut Cancellation:
  6/8 is the most severe; conditions to cancel:
  1. Both same nadi and both navamshas complement each other
  2. If H6 and H8 lords are mutual friends AND one is exalted
  Note: Even with cancellation attempts, 6/8 Bhakut remains challenging
```

## KUTA 8 — Nadi (Health, Genes & Pranic Compatibility) — Max 8 Points

> *"Samāna-nāḍī-dampaty-oḥ — santāna-kṣaya, ārogya-hāni,  
> Viyoga-bhaya, doṣa-pūrṇam vivāhyam."*  
> — **Muhurta Chintamani, Nadi Dosha Adhyaya**  
> *(When both partners have the same Nadi — there is risk to children's health,  
> physical ailments, fear of separation, and the marriage is full of dosha)*

```
NADI SYSTEM (3 channels of prana):
  Adi  (Vata/Air)    : Ashwini, Ardra, Punarvasu, Uttara Phalguni,
                       Hasta, Jyeshtha, Mula, Shatabhisha, P.Bhadra
  Madhya (Pitta/Fire): Bharani, Mrigashira, Pushya, P.Phalguni,
                       Chitra, Anuradha, P.Ashadha, Dhanishtha, U.Bhadra
  Antya (Kapha/Water): Krittika, Rohini, Ashlesha, Magha, Swati,
                       Vishakha, U.Ashadha, Shravana, Revati

NADI COMPATIBILITY SCORING:
  Different Nadi  = 8 points (full score; complementary pranas)
  Same Nadi       = 0 points (NADI DOSHA present)

NADI DOSHA EFFECTS (when same Nadi, no cancellation):
  Same Adi Nadi  = health problems for male partner
  Same Madhya    = health problems for female partner
  Same Antya     = health problems for both; progeny challenges

NADI DOSHA CANCELLATION CONDITIONS:
  Check these in order (each met condition reduces severity):

  1. Different Rashi (Moon signs different)       → Cancels dosha fully
     Source: MC Nadi Bhanga Sloka 3
  2. Different Nakshatra (despite same Nadi)       → Cancels dosha fully
     Source: MC Nadi Bhanga Sloka 4
  3. Same Nakshatra lord but different nakshatras  → Cancels by JR Ch.7 Sl.12
  4. Different Nakshatra pada (quarter)            → Partial mitigation
  5. Strong Jupiter in either chart               → Protective grace
  6. Both partners perform Nadi Dosha Shanti Puja  → Remedial cancellation

EXAMPLE — Ajay & Sravani:
  Ajay (Krittika, Pada 1) = ANTYA Nadi
  Sravani (U.Ashadha, Pada 1) = ANTYA Nadi
  SAME NADI → NADI DOSHA ⚠️ (raw score = 0)

  Cancellation check:
  1. Rashi different (Aries ≠ Sagittarius)      ✅ Condition 1 MET
  2. Nakshatra different (Krittika ≠ U.Ashadha)  ✅ Condition 2 MET
  3. Same nakshatra lord (both = SUN)             ✅ Condition 3 MET
  4. Same Pada (both Pada 1)                      ❌ NOT met
  5. Jupiter strong (Ajay: own sign H5)           ✅ Condition 5 MET

  4 of 5 conditions met → SIGNIFICANTLY MITIGATED
  Effective score: 4/8 (partial cancellation; remedies recommended)
```
