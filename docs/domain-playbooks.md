## Marriage Analysis — Five-Pillar Framework

For any reading that covers marriage/relationships, ALL 5 pillars must be assessed:

| Pillar | What to Analyze |
|--------|-----------------|
| 1. **7th House** | Sign, occupants, aspects. Malefics here → delays or complexity. Benefics → smoothness. |
| 2. **7th Lord** | House placement, dignity, aspects, retrograde, combustion. 7th lord in dusthāna → challenges. |
| 3. **Venus (men) / Jupiter (women)** | Natural kāraka for spouse. Dignity, house, aspects, combustion status. |
| 4. **Darakāraka (DK)** | Planet with lowest degree (Jaimini). DK's D9 sign and house → spouse characteristics and quality of marriage. |
| 5. **Upapada Lagna (UL)** | Ārūḍha of H12. UL sign, its lord, planets in UL/2nd from UL → nature and stability of marriage. |

**Marriage Timing** — apply the Five-Layer Transit Timing Protocol (`docs/interpretive-frameworks.md` §E) with:
- MD/AD of 7th lord, Venus, or DK must be active
- Jupiter must transit kendra/trikoṇa from 7th house or its lord
- Saturn must transit to activate the 7th axis

For deep-dive timing questions ("when will marriage happen," "what's blocking it," "how to fast-track it"), consult `Marriage_Guide_Part3.md` (Dasha/Transit/D9 Triple Agreement, Vivaha Yogas, Muhurta rules) and `Marriage_Guide_Part4.md` (partner qualities, synastry) in addition to this summary.

## Compatibility Analysis — Aṣṭakūṭa Framework

When comparing two charts for compatibility, apply the 8-kūṭa system:

| Kūṭa | Max Points | What It Measures |
|-------|-----------|------------------|
| 1. Varna | 1 | Spiritual compatibility (ego alignment) |
| 2. Vaśya | 2 | Mutual attraction and dominance |
| 3. Tārā (Dina) | 3 | Health and longevity of relationship |
| 4. Yoni | 4 | Physical/sexual compatibility |
| 5. Graha Maitri | 5 | Mental wavelength (Moon sign lords' relationship) |
| 6. Gaṇa | 6 | Temperament match (Deva/Mānuṣa/Rākṣasa) |
| 7. Bhakūṭa | 7 | Emotional and financial harmony |
| 8. Nāḍī | 8 | Genetic compatibility (health of children) |
| **Total** | **36** | **Minimum acceptable: 18/36** |

**Override rules**: Nāḍī doṣa is cancelled if bride and groom share the same nakṣatra but different pādas, or same rāśi but different nakṣatras. Bhakūṭa doṣa is cancelled if the lords of both Moon signs are friends or same planet.

## Birth Time Rectification Methods

When birth time accuracy is uncertain (fewer than 3/5 validation questions resonate), apply these methods:

1. **Daśā Boundary Mapping** — correlate major life events (marriage, first job, childbirth, parent's death) with MD/AD transition dates. Adjust birth time until daśā boundaries align with events.
2. **Tattva-based** — determine the ruling element (fire/earth/air/water) at the stated birth moment. Verify lagna degree falls in the correct tattva division.
3. **Praśna (Horary) Overlay** — cast a praśna chart for the moment of consultation. If the praśna lagna matches the natal lagna or its trikoṇa, birth time is likely accurate.
4. **Nāḍī Nāḍikā** — divide each nakṣatra into sub-divisions. Fine-tune Moon's degree to match known life events.
5. **Event-Driven Reverse Engineering** — take 3-5 known major events with exact dates. Back-calculate which lagna degree produces daśā/transit alignments matching all events simultaneously.

## Remedy Safety Rules

**CRITICAL — violations can cause harm:**

1. **NEVER prescribe a gemstone for a functional malefic.** Gemstones amplify planetary energy. Amplifying a malefic worsens outcomes. Always verify functional classification before any gem recommendation.
2. **Gemstone only after triple confirmation**: planet is (a) functional benefic for the Lagna, (b) weak/afflicted and needs strengthening, (c) not combust (combustion makes gem ineffective while Sun overpowers).
3. **Remedy priority order**: mantra → dāna (charity) → fasting → deity worship → gemstone → yantra → nāḍī-specific
4. **Mantras are always safe** — they align consciousness without amplifying malefic energy.
5. **Dāna is preferred over gems** for functional malefics — giving away the malefic planet's significations reduces its negative energy.
6. **Saturn remedies**: service to elderly/disabled, sesame oil offerings, iron charity. NEVER blue sapphire unless Saturn is yogakāraka (Taurus/Libra Lagna).
7. **Rahu remedies**: sandalwood, Durgā worship, abstinence from intoxicants. NEVER hessonite unless Rahu is well-placed and its dispositor is strong.
