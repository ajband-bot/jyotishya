## A. Three Lenses of Prediction

Every prediction must pass through all three lenses before being stated:

| Lens | Question | Source |
|------|----------|--------|
| **D1 Promise** | Does the rāśi chart show potential for this event? | House, lord, kāraka analysis |
| **D9 Confirmation** | Does the navāṁśa support or deny the D1 promise? | D9 sign, dignity, house of the relevant planet |
| **Daśā Timing** | Is there an active daśā/transit window to activate this promise? | MD/AD/PD lords + transit overlay |

**D1+D9 Interpretation Matrix** (BPHS Ch.65, Sārāvalī Ch.4):

| D1 State | D9 State | Interpretation |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| Strong (own/exalted/friend) | Strong | Promise fulfilled powerfully — best outcome |
| Strong | Weak (enemy/debilitated) | Promise undermined — expectations may not materialize fully |
| Weak (enemy/debilitated) | Strong | Hidden potential — late blooming, improvement after struggle |
| Weak | Weak | Genuine weakness — requires remedial support |

**Vargottama**: A planet in the same sign in D1 and D9 is Vargottama — treated as equivalent to own-sign strength. MUST be flagged in all planetary tables.

## B. Ten-Layer Synthesis Protocol

Every reading MUST synthesize through these 10 layers in order. No layer may be skipped.

| Layer | Name | What to Assess |
|-------|------|----------------|
| 1 | **Chart Vitality** | Lagna lord strength + Moon strength (mind) + Sun strength (health). Planets in kendras vs dusthānas count. This sets the baseline constitution. |
| 2 | **Lagneśa (Lagna Lord)** | House, sign, dignity, aspects, conjunctions, D9 position of the lagna lord. The single most important planet for the native's life direction. |
| 3 | **Planet Hierarchy** | Yogakāraka → functional benefics → functional neutrals → functional malefics → mārakas. Process planets in this order of importance. |
| 4 | **Yoga Identification** | Apply 8-Step Yoga Verification (§D below). Rate each yoga's strength and activation window. |
| 5 | **Dusthāna Analysis** | H6 (enemies/disease), H8 (transformation/longevity), H12 (loss/mokṣa). Which planets are placed here? Which lords are afflicted? |
| 6 | **Twelve Houses** | Systematically assess all 12 bhāvas: sign, lord, occupants, aspects, kāraka condition. One-line verdict per house. |
| 7 | **D9 Confirmation** | Re-assess all key findings through navāṁśa. Apply D1+D9 matrix. Check Darakāraka D9 placement for marriage. |
| 8 | **Daśā Timing** | Map current MD/AD/PD to life domains. Apply 5-Layer Daśā Reading (§C below). Historical daśā life-mapping for past event correlation. |
| 9 | **Transit Overlay** | Apply 5-Layer Transit Timing (§E below). Check Sade Sati status. Aṣṭakavarga bindus for transit strength. |
| 10 | **Final Verdict** | Synthesize all 9 layers. State predictions with confidence level. Flag any unresolved conflicts. |

## C. Five-Layer Daśā Reading Protocol

For every daśā lord (MD, AD, PD), assess through these 5 lenses:

| Layer | Question |
|-------|----------|
| 1. **Houses Owned** | Which houses does this planet lord over? Those life domains activate. |
| 2. **House Occupied** | Which house is it placed in? That domain's themes come into focus. |
| 3. **Dignity** | Exalted/own/friend → delivers results smoothly. Enemy/debilitated → delivers with struggle, delay, or distortion. |
| 4. **Aspects Received** | Benefic aspects enhance results. Malefic aspects (especially Saturn, Mars, Rahu) add obstacles or transform the outcome. |
| 5. **D9 Placement** | D9 sign and house of the daśā lord determines the deeper karmic quality of the period. Apply D1+D9 matrix. |

## D. Eight-Step Yoga Verification

No yoga may be claimed without passing ALL 8 steps:

1. **Identify candidate yoga** — name the yoga and its classical definition with verse reference
2. **Check exact planetary conditions** — which planets must be in which signs/houses? Verify against computed positions.
3. **Verify house positions** — are the planets in the correct houses as defined by the yoga?
4. **Check dignity of yoga-forming planets** — exalted/own-sign yogas are powerful; debilitated planets forming yogas are weak or may not manifest
5. **Check aspect/conjunction spoilers** — malefic aspects (Saturn, Mars, Rahu) on yoga-forming planets can spoil or delay activation
6. **Verify in D9** — if yoga-forming planets are weak in D9, the yoga's promise is undermined
7. **Assess daśā activation window** — which MD/AD will activate the yoga? If the relevant daśā is past or decades away, note this.
8. **Final strength rating** — Strong (all checks pass) / Moderate (minor spoilers) / Weak (significant impediments) / Dormant (no daśā activation in sight)

## E. Five-Layer Transit Timing Protocol

Events manifest when multiple transit layers converge. This replaces the simplified "Triple Agreement" with the full classical framework (SC Ch.6-7, BPHS Ch.65):

| Layer | Planet(s) | Window | Role |
|-------|-----------|--------|------|
| 1. **Karmic Window** | Saturn | ~2.5 years/sign | Defines when karma ripens. Saturn's transit over key houses (1, 4, 7, 10 from Moon/Lagna) creates the structural framework for events. |
| 2. **Opportunity Window** | Jupiter | ~12 months/sign | Defines when grace and growth activate. Jupiter's transit over trikoṇas (1, 5, 9) and kendras from Moon opens doors. |
| 3. **Karmic Catalyst** | Rahu/Ketu | ~18 months/sign | Sudden, unconventional, or past-life-driven events. Rahu/Ketu transiting over natal planets or key houses triggers obsessive energy. |
| 4. **Acute Trigger** | Mars | ~45 days/sign | Pinpoints when acute events manifest — surgery, conflict, accident, sudden initiative. Mars transit over sensitive houses triggers within the larger window. |
| 5. **Micro-Trigger** | Sun, Venus, Mercury | Days to weeks | Provides exact date for event manifestation within the windows set by Layers 1–4. |

**Convergence Rule**: A major life event (marriage, career change, health crisis, childbirth) requires:
- Daśā lord must promise the event (through houses owned/occupied)
- Saturn transit must activate the relevant house axis
- Jupiter transit must support (in kendra/trikoṇa from relevant house OR Moon)
- At least one fast-transit trigger (Mars/Sun/Venus) must fire

**Aṣṭakavarga Overlay**: When available, check the bindu score of the transit sign in the relevant planet's Bhinnaṣṭakavarga. Score ≥4 = favorable transit; ≤3 = obstructed transit.

## F. Six Questions per Planet

For every graha in the chart, the practitioner MUST answer these 6 questions:

1. **What houses does it own?** → determines which life domains it governs
2. **What house does it occupy?** → determines where it delivers results
3. **What is its dignity?** → exalted/own/mūlatrikoṇa/friend/neutral/enemy/debilitated
4. **What aspects does it receive?** → benefic vs malefic influence
5. **What conjunctions does it have?** → planetary combination effects
6. **Where is it in D9?** → soul-level confirmation or denial of D1 promise

## H. Special Yoga & Condition Checks

### Neechābhaṅga Rāja Yoga (Cancellation of Debilitation)
When a planet is debilitated, check these 5 conditions (BPHS Ch.28). If ANY condition is met, the debilitation is cancelled and the planet gains Rāja Yoga status:

1. The lord of the sign where the planet is debilitated is in a kendra from Lagna or Moon
2. The planet that gets exalted in the debilitation sign is in a kendra from Lagna or Moon
3. The lord of the debilitation sign aspects the debilitated planet
4. The debilitated planet is in a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10)
5. The debilitated planet is exalted in navāṁśa (D9)

### Kendrādhipati Doṣa
Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Moon) owning kendras (1, 4, 7, 10) lose their beneficence and become neutral or mildly malefic (BPHS Ch.34). This is especially significant when:
- Jupiter owns 7th for Gemini/Virgo Lagna
- Venus owns 7th for Aries/Scorpio Lagna
- This doṣa does NOT apply to natural malefics — they GAIN beneficence by owning kendras

### Parivartana Yoga (Mutual Exchange)
Two planets exchanging signs (each in the other's sign). Three types:

| Type | Condition | Effect |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **Mahā Parivartana** | Both planets in kendras (1,4,7,10) or trikoṇas (1,5,9) | Extremely auspicious — both houses greatly strengthened |
| **Khala Parivartana** | One planet in kendra/trikoṇa, one in dusthāna (3,6,8,12) | Mixed results — good planet dragged down, bad one elevated |
| **Dainya Parivartana** | Both involve dusthānas (6,8,12) | Inauspicious — suffering, debts, hidden enemies, health problems |

### Dispositor Chains
Follow the sign-lord chain for any planet: Planet → its sign lord → that lord's sign lord → until you reach a planet in own sign (the chain terminator) or a mutual exchange (loop). The chain terminator is the ultimate dispositor and holds hidden power over all planets in its chain.
