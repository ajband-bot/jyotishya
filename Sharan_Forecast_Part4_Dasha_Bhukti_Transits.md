## Foreword — How Time Speaks in Jyotiṣa

A horoscope is the *static map*. The **Vimśottarī daśā-bhukti-antara** is the *moving clock-hand* that activates portions of the map at specific dates. **Gocara (transits)** is the *weather of the day* — what is *currently* raining onto the map.

The result of any moment = (chart's natal promise) × (daśā-bhukti-antara activation) × (transit reinforcement). When all three align in the same direction, the result *manifests with full weight*. When they diverge, the result is *muted, deferred, or partial*.
