## 1️⃣ The Daily Five-Minute Foundation (Non-Negotiable)

Even on the busiest day, these five practices take **5 minutes total** and form the *unbroken thread* of the chart's blessing-flow.

### 1.1 At Sunrise (or first morning)
- **Sūrya-praṇāma** — face East, fold hands, look towards rising Sun (or its direction if cloudy), 30 seconds of silent acknowledgement
- Mentally chant: **"Oṁ Ādityāya namaḥ"** — 11 times
- *Activates*: D24 Sun-uccha (education-fame), D60 Sun-svakṣetra (authority), neutralises D9 + D45 Sun-debility curriculum

### 1.2 After morning ablutions / before work
- **"Oṁ Saravaṇa-bhavāya namaḥ"** — 28 times (Subrahmanya's mūla-mantra)
- *Activates*: Iṣṭa-devatā signature (12th-from-Karakāṁśa = Mars+Ketu in D9 = Subrahmanya)

### 1.3 At sunset / dusk
- **Light a small lamp** (oil or ghee) at home altar — 30 seconds of silent presence
- Soft chant: **"Oṁ Hanumate namaḥ"** — 11 times
- *Activates*: Lagna-nakṣatra Dhaniṣṭhā (Aṣṭa-Vasus, ruler Mars) + Hanumān as accessible Mars-Ketu Iṣṭa-form + Sandhyā discipline (D20 Saturn-svakṣetra responds)

### 1.4 Before any meal
- 3 seconds: hands joined, mental "Oṁ Annapūrṇāyai namaḥ" or any food-blessing
- *Activates*: gratitude reflex which Saturn (lagneśa) honours; also Annapūrṇa = Mother-energy = D40 maternal-vargottama

### 1.5 Before sleep
- **Last thought**: "Oṁ Nārāyaṇāya namaḥ" — *one* time, just before eyes close
- *Activates*: D9 Venus-exalted-in-Pisces (Nārāyaṇa's sign); marriage-protection; deepest-dharmik-rest; D45 paternal-line karma-purification

**Total time**: 5 minutes. **Power**: cumulative — over a year = 30 hours of structured *graha-deva-prīṇana*; over a decade = 300 hours = a *complete* puraścaraṇa-equivalent.
