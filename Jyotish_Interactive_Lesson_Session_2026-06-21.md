## Core Method Reinforced

Intermediate Jyotiṣa requires synthesis, not isolated planet keywords.

Every prediction must consider:
1. House placement — where the graha acts
2. Sign/dignity — how strong or comfortable it is
3. Lordship — what houses it controls functionally
4. Aspects/conjunctions — what modifies it
5. Divisional confirmation, especially D9 for marriage/dharma
6. Daśā/transit timing — when the promise activates

Memory hook:

> Lord of X in Y = X expresses through Y.
