## STEP 14 — Ideal Partner Qualities from the Natal Chart

> *"Patni-guṇān vijānīyat saptameśa-śukrābhyām;  
> Nākṣatrāt, rāśīśvarāt, kārakasya balaad api."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.78, Patni-Guṇa Adhyāya**  
> *(Know the spouse's qualities from the 7th lord, Venus, the nakshatra,  
> the Moon sign's lord, and the karaka's strength)*

### 14.1 The 9-Layer Partner Profile

```
For a MALE chart (♂), build the partner profile from:
  LAYER 1: H7 SIGN nature → core personality type of spouse
  LAYER 2: H7 SIGN's ELEMENT (fire/earth/air/water) → elemental temperament
  LAYER 3: PLANETS IN H7 → what those planets inject into the spouse's character
  LAYER 4: H7 LORD sign & house → the arena where spouse's energy operates
  LAYER 5: H7 LORD nakshatra → sub-theme of spouse identity
  LAYER 6: VENUS sign & house → what type of woman the native desires/attracts
  LAYER 7: VENUS nakshatra → deeper layer of desired partner quality
  LAYER 8: DARAKARAKA (lowest degree planet) → the karmic type of spouse
  LAYER 9: D9 7th house (Navamsha 7th) → the SOUL-LEVEL quality of the partner

For a FEMALE chart (♀), replace Venus with JUPITER in layers 6-7.
```

## STEP 15 — Cross-Chart Synastry Analysis

> *"Vara-sūrye kanyā-candre same rāśāu — ātma-manas-aikyam;

> *(When the groom's Sun is in the same sign as the bride's Moon —  
> it is a union of soul and mind; the couple experiences the highest marital happiness)*

### 15.1 The 7 Key Synastry Indicators

```
SYNASTRY INDICATOR 1 — Sun-Moon Cross Aspect (Most Important)
  Groom Sun same sign as Bride Moon = HIGHEST compatibility
  Bride Sun same sign as Groom Moon = Very good; she understands him
  Sun-Moon in trine (1/5/9) = excellent intuitive understanding
  Sun-Moon in square (1/4/7/10) = tension but magnetic attraction
  Sun-Moon in 6/8 = friction; significant adjustments required

SYNASTRY INDICATOR 2 — Moon-Moon Relationship
  Same sign = too similar; may compete or suffocate each other
  1/7 (opposition) = complementary; see each other clearly
  5/9 (trine) = harmonious; natural understanding
  3/11 = friendly; shared goals
  2/12 or 6/8 = tension; requires work

SYNASTRY INDICATOR 3 — Lagna-to-Lagna Relationship
  5/9 Trikona = BEST (dharmic alignment + progeny blessings)
  3/11 = friendly, growing together
  1/7 = complementary opposition; strong attraction
  2/12 = money/value differences; intimate but financially strained
  4/10 = career vs home pull; power dynamics
  6/8 = adversarial; one partner may drain the other

SYNASTRY INDICATOR 4 — Groom's Venus vs Bride's Moon
  Venus conjunct or trine Moon = natural love, ease, harmony
  Venus 7th from Moon = complementary polarity; attraction across difference

SYNASTRY INDICATOR 5 — Bride's Jupiter vs Groom's Moon
  Jupiter conjunct or trine Moon = wisdom nourishes the groom's emotional world
  Jupiter in same sign as Moon = husband feels blessed and expanded

SYNASTRY INDICATOR 6 — Lagna Lord Cross-Relationship
  Groom's Lagna lord + Bride's Lagna lord = natural friends?
  Friendly = fundamental personality compatibility (daily harmony)
  Enemy = fundamental personality clash (underlying tension always)

SYNASTRY INDICATOR 7 — 7th Lord Cross-Analysis
  Groom's H7 lord and Bride's H7 lord: are they friendly?
  Friendly = both parties' marriage expectations are mutually satisfying
  Enemy = each expects different things from marriage; chronic friction
```

### 17.1 The 10-Layer Marriage Compatibility Protocol

```
LAYER 1: ASHTAKUTA SCORE (after dosha cancellations)
  <18 = problematic; 18-24 = workable; 25+ = excellent
  Weight: 20% of total assessment

LAYER 2: GRAHA MAITRI (Moon sign lord friendship)
  0-2 = problematic; 3-4 = workable; 5 = ideal
  Weight: Highest individual kuta; can override low total score

LAYER 3: NADI & BHAKUT DOSHA STATUS
  Both absent = ideal; one present with cancellation = acceptable;
  Both present uncancelled = significant concern
  Weight: Can block even good scores if uncancelled

LAYER 4: MANGAL DOSHA STATUS
  Both Manglik = mutually cancelled = positive;
  One Manglik (uncancelled) = serious concern;
  Both non-Manglik = neutral
  Weight: Moderate; single most talked-about dosha

LAYER 5: LAGNA COMPATIBILITY (5/9 trikona vs 6/8 axis)
  5/9 = excellent; 3/11 = good; 1/7 = complementary; 6/8 = challenging
  Weight: Important extra-Kuta factor

LAYER 6: SUN-MOON SYNASTRY CROSS-ASPECT
  Groom Sun + Bride Moon: same sign = highest; trine = excellent;
  square = workable; 6/8 = friction
  Weight: Second highest extra-Kuta factor

LAYER 7: LAGNA LORD FRIENDSHIP
  Mutual friends = fundamental harmony; enemies = fundamental clash
  Weight: Determines daily baseline of relationship quality

LAYER 8: 7TH LORD FRIENDSHIP (between charts)
  Friendly = mutual marital expectations met;
  enemies = chronic frustration of expectations
  Weight: Important for long-term satisfaction

LAYER 9: D9 CROSS-COMPATIBILITY
  Moon-Moon 7th mirror = exceptional inner understanding;
  Jupiter-Jupiter 7th mirror = complementary wisdom;
  Same planet exalted in both = shared inner strength
  Weight: Reveals subtle/spiritual compatibility level

LAYER 10: DASHA SYNCHRONISATION
  Both in auspicious, mutually supportive Dasha periods
  = relationship materializes with ease;
  Both in challenging Dashas = relationship faces tests but shared journey
  Weight: Timing indicator for when relationship will thrive
```
