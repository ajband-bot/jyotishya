## STEP 14 — Dosha Identification & Cancellation

> *"Doṣa-śāntim kuryāt śāstra-vidhina — doṣa-jñānam-apūrvakam."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.93** *(Know the dosha first; then prescribe the remedy)*

A **Dosha** is an inherent flaw, imbalance, or karmic burden in a chart that can suppress blessings. The expert **checks every dosha systematically**, verifies ALL cancellation conditions, and only then assigns severity. A dosha pronounced without checking cancellations is an incomplete — and often wrong — reading.

### 14.1 Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha)

**Rule**:
> *"Vyaye sukhe vā pātāle jāmike vā kalaṭrare;  
> Dvādāśeca sthāne kuje — kuja-doṣaḥ prakīrtitaḥ."*  
> — **Jataka Parijata Ch.9** and **Muhurta Chintamani**

```
Mangal Dosha is present when Mars occupies:
  H1 (Lagna)   — house of self
  H2 (Dhana)   — house of family
  H4 (Sukha)   — house of home
  H7 (Kalatra) — house of spouse
  H8 (Āyu)    — house of longevity
  H12 (Vyaya)  — house of losses

Check from THREE reference points:
  1. From Lagna (Ascendant)
  2. From Moon  (Chandra Lagna)
  3. From Venus (Shukra — specifically for marriage compatibility)
```

**Cancellation Conditions** (any single one cancels the dosha):

| 6 | Mars in H7 for Cancer or Leo Lagna | Traditional authority |

| 8 | Mars in H12 for Taurus or Libra Lagna | Traditional authority |
| 9 | Jupiter fully aspects Mars | Jyotish Ratnakar Ch.5 |
| 10 | Partner is also Manglik | JR: *"Ubhayoh kuja-doṣa-yukte — doṣa-kṣayaḥ"* |
| 11 | Mars conjoins Jupiter or full Moon | Sarvartha Chintamani |

### 15.2 Navamsha (D9) — Calculation Formula

```
Each zodiac sign (30°) divides into 9 equal Navamsha padas of 3°20′ (3.3333°).
Navamsha start sign depends on D1 sign type:

  Movable signs  (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn)   : starts from ARIES
  Fixed signs    (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius)     : starts from CAPRICORN
  Dual signs     (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) : starts from LIBRA

FORMULA:
  pada_index   = FLOOR(degree_in_sign / 3.3333)         [gives 0–8]
  start_index  = {Aries:0, Capricorn:9, Libra:6}
  D9_sign      = SIGNS[(start_index + pada_index) mod 12]
```

## STEP 16 — Vimshottari Dasha Calculation

> *"Vimśottari-daśā śreṣṭhā Kali-yuge — nakṣatra-krama-phala-dāyinī."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.46** *(Vimshottari Dasha is the supreme timing system in Kali Yuga)*

### 16.1 The 120-Year Cycle

```
Total Vimshottari cycle = 120 years across 9 lords:
  Sun       =  6 yrs   Moon     = 10 yrs   Mars     =  7 yrs
  Rahu      = 18 yrs   Jupiter  = 16 yrs   Saturn   = 19 yrs
  Mercury   = 17 yrs   Ketu     =  7 yrs   Venus    = 20 yrs

Fixed sequence: Sun → Moon → Mars → Rahu → Jupiter → Saturn
                → Mercury → Ketu → Venus → (repeats from Sun)

Starting lord determined by Moon’s nakshatra at birth:
  Nak 1,10,19 (Ashwini,Magha,Mula)           → Ketu
  Nak 2,11,20 (Bharani,P.Phalguni,P.Ashadha) → Venus
  Nak 3,12,21 (Krittika,U.Phalguni,U.Ashadha)→ Sun
  Nak 4,13,22 (Rohini,Hasta,Shravana)        → Moon
  Nak 5,14,23 (Mrigashira,Chitra,Dhanishtha) → Mars
  Nak 6,15,24 (Ardra,Swati,Shatabhisha)      → Rahu
  Nak 7,16,25 (Punarvasu,Vishakha,P.Bhadra)  → Jupiter
  Nak 8,17,26 (Pushya,Anuradha,U.Bhadra)     → Saturn
  Nak 9,18,27 (Ashlesha,Jyeshtha,Revati)     → Mercury
```

### 16.2 Calculation Step-by-Step

```
STEP A: Find elapsed fraction of nakshatra at birth:
  Elapsed fraction = (Moon degrees into nakshatra) / 13.3333°

STEP B: Compute remaining first Dasha period:
  Remaining = (1 − Elapsed fraction) × Dasha lord’s total years

STEP C: Sequence forward:
  After remaining period → next lord’s FULL period → next → etc.

STEP D: Antardasha within each Mahadasha:
  AD_duration = (AD_lord_years / 120) × MD_lord_years
  Sequence: Start from MD lord itself, then proceed in fixed order
```

### 16.5 The 5-Layer Dasha Reading Protocol

For any active Dasha period (MD + AD), assess the lord through:

```
  LAYER 1: HOUSE OWNED by Dasha lord → those house-themes ACTIVATE
  LAYER 2: HOUSE OCCUPIED by Dasha lord → the field where events play out
  LAYER 3: DIGNITY of Dasha lord → exalted/own = full; debil/combust = reduced
  LAYER 4: ASPECTS received by Dasha lord → benefic = support; malefic = challenge
  LAYER 5: D9 PLACEMENT of Dasha lord → inner quality of the period’s results
```

### 17.1 Transit Hierarchy of Importance

```
Most important transit references (in order):
  1. FROM NATAL MOON SIGN (primary reference for all timing)
```

### 17.4 Ashtakavarga — Transit Point Scoring

> *"Aṣṭākavargaṃ — grahāṇāṃ kṣetra-bala-nirdhaāraṇāya."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.66**

```
CONCEPT:
  Each house in the natal chart accumulates "bindus" (points) from
  all 8 reference planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus,
  Saturn, Ascendant).

  Score range per house: 0–8 points per planet = max 56 total
  (Sarvashtakavarga uses all planets combined; max = 337 across 12 houses)

  Transit through house with HIGH Bindus (25+) = positive outcomes
  Transit through house with LOW Bindus (<25)  = challenging period

PRACTICAL USE:
  Before predicting an event, check the Sarvashtakavarga score of the
  relevant house. A poor score confirms difficulty; a rich score confirms
  the Dasha/transit prediction of gain.
```
