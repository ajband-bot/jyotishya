*"Rāśi-phalaṁ sthūlaṁ — aṁśa-phalaṁ sūkṣmam."*
*(The Rāśi chart gives broad results; the divisional charts reveal the subtle and specific.)*
— Traditional Jyotisha maxim

The divisional charts (Varga or Aṁśa) are the microscopes of Jyotisha. Where the D1 Rāśi chart reveals the broad canvas of the soul's journey, the vargas zoom into specific departments of life with extraordinary precision. BPHS devotes entire chapters to their analysis, and Maharishi Parāśara is categorical: no judgment should be passed on any specific life domain — wealth, marriage, career, children — without examining the relevant varga.
