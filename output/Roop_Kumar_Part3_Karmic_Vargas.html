The D20 reveals the form of devotional practice most natural and effective for this jīva. Every chart has a unique spiritual fingerprint — the D20 reads it.

D20 Key Positions

Moon in Svātī (Vāyu-devatā) D20 influence: The breath is a direct spiritual tool. Prāṇāyāma (especially nāḍī-śodhana — alternate nostril breathing) before morning meditation produces rapid centering. Svātī's wind-nature means: breath-work is your #1 pre-meditation practice.

Rāhu in 9th (Śravaṇa = "the Listener") echoes here: the deepest learning comes through listening — podcasts, discourses, śravaṇa of texts. Then manana (reflection through writing). Then nididhyāsana (abiding in the understanding).
