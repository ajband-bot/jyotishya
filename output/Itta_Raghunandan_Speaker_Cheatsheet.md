# Speaker Cheat Sheet — Itta Raghunandan
*For the person delivering this reading out loud. Not for distribution — this is your prep notes.*

---

## 1. The One-Sentence Mental Model (say this if you remember nothing else)

**"Raghunandan's chart is an engine room, not a jackpot — dharma and career are wired together through a Mercury-Venus exchange, and the tools to fix what looks broken (debilitated Venus) are already installed. Nothing here is luck. Everything here rewards patient use of what's already present."**

If you only have 60 seconds with him, say that sentence, then stop talking and let him ask a question.

---

## 2. Suggested Conversation Order (story arc, not house-by-house recitation)

Do **not** walk through the chart in H1→H12 order out loud — that's how it reads on paper, not how it should sound in a room. Use this arc instead:

1. **Open on the strongest, most flattering true thing first.** Venus as yogakāraka (H5+H10), the Mercury-Venus exchange. Let him feel the chart likes him before you introduce any tension.
2. **Introduce Jupiter's Hamsa Yoga as capacity, not certainty.** "You have real capacity for wisdom and being sought out for counsel — what you do with that capacity is still yours to build."
3. **Talk about Saturn/Lagna-lord placement as identity, not fate.** Frame the 3rd-house Saturn as "you were built for sustained effort, not overnight wins" — a compliment about character, not a sentence.
4. **Only now, gently, name the 9th-house pressure.** Never lead with this. Never use the word "doṣa" unless he asks a direct follow-up question that needs it.
5. **Close on the dharma path and a concrete practice.** End every session on something he can *do*, not something he now has to *worry about*.

## 4. Exact Phrases — Use vs. Avoid

**Avoid saying:**
- "Guaranteed" / "definitely will happen"
- "Your debilitation is completely resolved" (say "substantially repaired" instead)
- "Pitṛ Doṣa" as a flat diagnosis (say "pressure on the 9th house" unless he specifically wants the technical term, then explain the hedge honestly)
- "Retrograde planets give delayed but complete success" (this was flagged as an overreach — don't repeat it)
- Any specific date as a promise ("you will get promoted in March 2028") — always frame windows as *elevated likelihood*, not appointments

**Use instead:**
- "Tends to..." / "is a strong candidate for..." / "classical texts read this as..."
- "Worth watching during [period]..."
- "This is a real strength, though how it plays out is still shaped by your own choices."
- "I want to be honest with you about something that needs attention, alongside something that helps it."

## 5. Handling the Hard Stuff — Sample Script

**On the 9th-house pressure (father/teachers/dharma):**
> "There's a part of your chart that shows real tension around the 9th house — the house of father, teachers, and your sense of higher meaning. I want to be straightforward: this isn't a curse, and I'm not going to call it something scarier than the evidence supports. What I can say confidently is that your relationship to authority figures, tradition, or your own sense of purpose may ask more conscious effort from you than it does for some people. That's worth naming honestly — and it comes with a practice, not just a warning."

**On Maṅgala Doṣa (if he asks about it):**
> "Yes, there's a classical combination present. But cancellation rules for this vary a lot between schools of thought, and I don't want to give you a false sense of certainty either way — present or cancelled. What I'd rather do is look at your actual marriage timing through the daśā and transit windows, which tell us more than the doṣa label alone."

## 6. Anticipated Questions & How to Answer

| He might ask... | Answer with... |
|---|---|
| "So is my father okay? Is something wrong with my dad?" | "This isn't a prediction about his health or safety — it's about the *relationship* and *inherited themes*, which is a very different thing. Worth an honest conversation with him if anything feels unresolved, not worry about his wellbeing." |
| "When exactly will I get married / promoted?" | Give the daśā/transit window as a *probability-weighted season*, not a date: "The [X] period is where multiple signals converge — that's when I'd watch closely, not treat as an appointment." |
| "Is Jupiter good or bad in my chart?" | "Both, in different ways — dignified and capable (Hamsa Yoga), but also carrying some houses that ask more of you when it comes to partnerships. Strength and effort aren't opposites here." |
| "Why did an earlier version of this reading say something different?" | Be direct: "I reviewed it against a second expert opinion and found a few places where the language was more certain than the evidence supported — I've corrected those. That's normal, healthy practice, not something to worry about." |

## 8. Delivery Tips

- **Pace:** slow down noticeably when you get to any caution/dosha material. Rushing through hard news reads as evasive; lingering calmly reads as trustworthy.
- **Tone:** warm and matter-of-fact, never dramatic. If you wouldn't say it to a friend over coffee, don't say it in the reading.
- **Always pair a hard truth with something actionable.** Never let a caution be the last thing you say about a topic.
- **End on dharma path + one practice**, every single time, no exceptions — that's the emotional exit ramp.
- **If he pushes for more certainty than the chart supports, it's okay to say "I don't have enough signal to say that with confidence."** That's not a weak answer — it's the whole discipline.
