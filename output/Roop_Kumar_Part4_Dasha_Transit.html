Mercury Mahādaśā (2021–2038) — The 17-Year Reading

Mercury owns the 2nd (wealth, speech, family) and 5th (intellect, children, pūrva-puṇya) houses — both benefic for Taurus lagna. As a trikona lord (H5), Mercury is a functional benefic. The challenge: Mercury is combust at 1.52° AND retrograde in the 7th house.

What Mercury MD means practically:

  • This 17-year period is about harvesting the Saturn-MD platform through communication
  • Career authority built in 2002-2021 now needs to be articulated, documented, taught, shared
  • The combustion means communication requires conscious effort — not because you lack brilliance, but because the transmission needs polishing
  • Mercury retrograde = the best insights come through revision, editing, second-drafts — not first attempts
  • Partnership themes remain active (Mercury sits in H7) — professional partnerships carry financial fruit

Venus Antardaśā (Jan 2025 – Nov 2027) — Current Sub-Period

Venus is the Lagneśa — ruler of self. Venus AD within Mercury MD activates:

  • Self-renewal: the identity undergoes a positive transformation (Venus in 8th = renovating the self)
  • Health improvement: Venus as lagneśa in 8th = the body's hidden reserves are accessed
  • Financial shifts: Venus rules H6 (service-income) too — income through service channels
  • Spiritual deepening: Venus in Pūrvāṣāḍhā (Venus's own nakṣatra in Sagittarius) = the seeker finds beauty in the teaching

Net Venus AD verdict: FAVORABLE — This is one of the best sub-periods within Mercury MD, because the Lagneśa supports the self directly.

Rahu Pratyantardaśā (Jan 12 – Jun 16, 2026) — Current Sub-Sub

🌟 Critical Transit Events

1. Jupiter enters Cancer (EXALTATION) ~June 12-15, 2026

Jupiter moves into Cancer — its sign of exaltation — and enters H3 from Lagna (courage, communication, initiative) and H10 from Moon (career, karma). This is a once-in-12-years event and carries extraordinary blessing:

  • From Moon: Jupiter in 10th = career uplift, recognition, dharmic work blessed
  • From Lagna: Jupiter in 3rd = courage to communicate, write, teach — exactly what Mercury MD needs!
  • Jupiter exalted aspects: 7th (H9 from Lagna = bhāgya!), 9th (H11 = gains!), 5th (H7 = marriage blessed!)

This transit runs until ~June 2027. The entire year ahead is Jupiter-exalted blessed.

2. Mars transits natal Lagna (Taurus) ~July 1-August 10, 2026

Mars (H7+H12 lord) transiting the Lagna brings:

  • Energy surge — physical vitality high; initiative capacity at peak
  • Caution: Mars is a māraka + vyaya lord for Taurus → health vigilance needed
  • Mars transits over natal Saturn's position (~Jul 8-15) → Saturn-Mars conjunction in transit = high tension potential. Avoid confrontations, risky travel, surgical procedures in this window unless essential
  • Channel the Mars energy into exercise, project execution, structured initiative

3. Rāhu in Aquarius (H10) — throughout the period

Rāhu transiting the 10th house (career) is a major career-transformation indicator:

  • Career may undergo unconventional shifts — new roles, unexpected opportunities, technology-driven changes
  • Rāhu in 10th amplifies ambition — guard against over-reaching
  • Rāhu transiting natal Mars's sign (Aquarius) where natal Mars sits = activation of the career-Mars
  • Remedy: Before any major career decision, pause 48 hours. Rāhu creates urgency that may not be real.

4. Mercury Retrograde ~late Jun to late Jul 2026

Transit Mercury goes retrograde in Cancer/Gemini (H3/H2 from Lagna). For a native already in Mercury MD with natal Mercury retrograde+combust, this period requires:

  • Triple-check all written communications, contracts, and financial documents
  • Do not launch new projects during Mercury retro — instead, revise, edit, refine
  • This is an excellent period for inner reflection, spiritual reading, and journal-writing
