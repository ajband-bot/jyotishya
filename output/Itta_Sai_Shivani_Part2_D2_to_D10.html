1. Why We Read Multiple Vargas

The Rāśi (D1) chart shows the raw material of a life. Each divisional chart (varga) re-slices the same birth moment through a different classical lens — D2 for wealth, D3 for courage/siblings, D4 for home/property, D7 for children, D9 for marriage-depth (a partial recap, since Part 1 already introduced it), and D10 for career. A finding that repeats across several vargas is far more reliable than one that appears only once.

6. D9 (Navāṁśa) — Marriage Depth

Part 1 introduced your D9 findings for chart-strength purposes (Neechābhaṅga, hidden strengths). Here we look specifically at what the D9 says about marriage and partnership.

Observation: Your D9 Lagna is Gemini (Vargottama, per Part 1) — the 7th house from the D9 Lagna is Sagittarius, occupied by a debilitated Rāhu. Your natural marriage-significator Venus sits in Aquarius (D9 9th house, a trikoṇa). Jupiter — your D1 7th lord — is exalted in the D9 (Cancer, 2nd house).

Interpretation: A debilitated Rāhu in the D9's own 7th house suggests some idealization, illusion, or an unconventional quality to how partnership is approached or experienced — worth naming honestly rather than glossing over. Venus in a trikoṇa is a stabilizing counter-balance. Jupiter's D9 exaltation (matching the D1 finding from Part 1) reconfirms that despite the D1's Kendrādhipati Doṣa surface complexity, the deeper marriage promise is genuinely strong — this is the "delay, not denial" pattern named in Part 1, now confirmed a second time from the D9's own dedicated partnership angle.
