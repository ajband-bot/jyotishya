Flagging here so anyone referencing an earlier cached copy of either document knows to re-pull the current version.

Lesson for future readings: always cross-verify Arūḍha hand-calculations against the codebase's arudha_pada() function rather than computing "distance" informally — the wrap-around direction is easy to get backwards.
