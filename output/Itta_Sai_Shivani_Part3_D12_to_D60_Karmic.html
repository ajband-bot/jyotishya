5. Kārakāṁśa & Iṣṭa Devatā

Your Kārakāṁśa (the navāṁśa sign occupied by your Ātmakāraka Sun) is Sagittarius — a fire sign ruled by Jupiter, the guru-planet. In Jaimini practice, the dispositor of the Kārakāṁśa is one of the strongest classical indicators of the Iṣṭa Devatā (chosen deity best suited to one's soul path).

Cross-checking multiple methods, as this reading always does rather than favoring one silently:

  • Kārakāṁśa dispositor (Jupiter, primary method): Points toward a Viṣṇu-Nārāyaṇa or Guru-linked devatā — Jupiter is the classical significator of this divine principle.
  • Lagna Nakṣatra deity (Aditi, Punarvasu): Mother of the Ādityas, including Viṣṇu in his solar aspect — a complementary, reinforcing thread rather than a contradiction.
  • Ātmakāraka's own D1 nakṣatra deity (Aryaman, Uttara Phalgunī): God of contracts/alliances — points toward a devotional practice with a partnership or covenant-oriented flavor (fitting, given the marriage-linked findings throughout this reading).
  • 9th lord Saturn's nakṣatra deity (Yama, Bharaṇī): A justice/dharma-oriented undertone, fitting Saturn's role as your currently-running Mahādaśā lord.

Primary recommendation: A Viṣṇu-Nārāyaṇa-oriented devotional practice (Jupiter/Guru-linked) is the strongest-supported candidate, converging from two independent methods (Kārakāṁśa dispositor and Lagna Nakṣatra deity). This does not exclude family or personal devotional traditions already in place — Iṣṭa Devatā determination refines and confirms existing inclination rather than overriding it. If your family already worships a specific Viṣṇu form (Veṅkaṭeśvara, Nārāyaṇa, Rāma, Kṛṣṇa), this finding supports continuing and deepening that practice specifically during your current Saturn Mahādaśā.

Across every karmic layer examined in this Part — parents, comforts, spirituality, education, innate character, and past-life karma itself — the same honest pattern repeats: real substance, real complexity, and a consistent, repeatable path toward resolution through patient, disciplined, values-aligned effort. Nothing here points to fatalism; everything points to a life that rewards conscious cultivation.
