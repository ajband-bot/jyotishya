Foreword — Why Divisional Charts Matter

"Varge varge graha-phalaṁ pṛthak-pṛthak vicintayet;

Sarva-vargottamaḥ śreṣṭho, balavān eva sarvadā."

— BPHS, Adhyāya 6, śloka 11

(For each divisional chart, the results of the planets must be considered separately. One who is Vargottama in all divisions is the most excellent and powerful in every regard.)

The D1 rāśi chart is the body of a life — its skeleton, its gross form. The vargas are the organs: each one governs a specific domain of existence with a precision that the rāśi chart alone cannot offer. A planet weak in D1 but honoured across its decisive vargas may still command its domain with quiet authority. A planet strong in D1 but fallen in its primary varga must be watched — the D1 promise may erode precisely where it matters most.

Gayathri's chart carries one of the most extraordinary varga-signatures I have encountered in decades of study: a Vimala Vipareeta Rāja Yoga through the Lagneśa, a Mahā Parivartana between career and intelligence, and a Vargottama Mars that serves as the bedrock anchor across D1 and D9. How these themes express across the divisional layers — that is what Part 2 exists to reveal.

The D9 section is intentionally the longest. Parāśara himself states that no result from the D1 should be pronounced without first consulting the Navāṁśa. Here, we shall dwell with particular care.

1. D2 — Horā (Wealth Polarity)

1.1 Method

Each rāśi divides into two horās of 15 degrees each. Odd signs (Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius): 0°–15° falls in Sūrya's horā (Leo); 15°–30° falls in Candra's horā (Cancer). Even signs (Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces): the polarity reverses — 0°–15° is Candra's horā (Cancer); 15°–30° is Sūrya's horā (Leo). The D2 reveals the fundamental polarity of wealth: whether it flows through solar channels (authority, recognition, government, directed leadership) or lunar channels (service, the public, recurring income, care-giving, emotional intelligence).

7. D9 — Navāṁśa: The Soul Chart (Dharma-Varga)

The Navāṁśa is the most important divisional chart in the entire Parāśarī system. No result from the rāśi chart should be pronounced without first consulting the Navāṁśa — Mahārṣi Parāśara himself insists on this in BPHS Adhyāya 6. The D9 is the dharma-varga, the soul's true constitution, the inner blueprint that determines whether D1 promises are fulfilled or frustrated, and the primary lens for marriage, spiritual orientation, and soul-level planetary strength. Where D1 shows the gross body of fate, D9 shows its subtle architecture.

"Rāśi-phalaṁ vapuḥ-sthāne, navāṁśe tu phalaṁ pṛthak;

Rāśi-navāṁśānusāreṇa pūrṇa-phala-viniścayaḥ."

— BPHS, Adhyāya 6, śloka 4

(The rāśi gives the result at the level of the physical body of events; the navāṁśa gives the result at the soul level. Full determination of fruits requires examining both together.)
