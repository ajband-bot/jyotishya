7. Upapada Lagna — Marriage & Partner Signature

Two valid classical methods for computing the Upapada (marriage indicator) give different results for this chart — both are presented here rather than silently choosing one, per this practice's transparency commitment:

  • Primary (canonical Ārūḍha-distance method): Aquarius. Suggests a spouse tendency toward independent-mindedness, intellectual engagement, and comfort with an unconventional path within the relationship.
  • Alternate (inclusive-count method, used in some regional traditions): Gemini. Suggests a more communicative, versatile, dual-natured spousal temperament.

Both readings converge on one point worth noting with more confidence: the marriage partner is unlikely to be a passive, purely conventional figure — a picture consistent with the exalted, kendra-seated Jupiter (Haṁsa Yoga) governing the 7th house directly.
