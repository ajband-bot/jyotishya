Part 2 of 5 — The Divisional Charts, D2 through D10

Itta Madhavi

Where the D1 shows the raw promise, the divisional charts (vargas) reveal how each life-theme is refined and confirmed — wealth, siblings, home, children, marriage, and career, each seen through its own dedicated lens.

1. Why Divisional Charts Matter

The D1 (Rāśi) chart shows the body and overall life; each varga beyond it (D2, D3, D4...) subdivides every sign further and re-examines a specific life domain with sharper resolution. A promise seen in D1 that is confirmed in its relevant varga is considered reliable; one that is contradicted deserves nuance rather than denial. This practice's current calculators cover D2, D3, D4, D7, D9, and D10 fully — D5 (Pañcāṁśa) and D6 (Ṣaṣṭhāṁśa) are not yet implemented in this system and are honestly reported as a data gap rather than guessed at.

2. D2 Hora — Wealth

Your D2 Lagna is Leo. The most striking feature of this chart is an extraordinary concentration: eight of your nine grahas — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, and Ketu — all land together in Cancer, in the 12th house from this D2 Lagna. Only Venus stands apart, alone in Leo, in the D2 Lagna itself.

Observation: Eight of nine grahas cluster in D2 Cancer/H12, including Jupiter exalted and Moon in its own sign there; Venus alone occupies the D2 Lagna.

Classical principle: The D2 examines wealth and its sources specifically; a 12th-house concentration in this chart traditionally suggests wealth linked to foreign connections, quiet accumulation, spending through or for others, or resources that are not always visibly displayed.

Interpretation: Despite the 12th-house placement, an exalted Jupiter and own-sign Moon within that same cluster suggest this is not a wealth-depleting signature but rather one of hidden, stored, or quietly-growing resources — wealth that may not be the most visible or showy, but which has real depth and even a nourishing quality (Moon) and wisdom-linked growth (Jupiter) behind it. Venus standing alone in the D2 Lagna, meanwhile, suggests your personal relationship to money and values is comparatively more visible, direct, and self-defined than the "hidden" collective placement of the other eight.

Prediction: This is offered as an indicative tendency — wealth accumulation via quieter, longer-horizon means (savings, property, family resources, or connections through partnership/foreign links) rather than sudden windfalls, best read together with the daśā timing in Part 4.

3. D3 Drekkāṇa — Siblings & Courage

Your D3 Lagna is Virgo, and Mercury — the D3 Lagna's own ruler — sits exalted directly within it. This is a clean, strong signature for courage, self-effort, and sibling-related matters: a capacity for precise, capable, even exceptional self-application when called upon. Mars and Saturn again appear paired together here (in the 11th house of this chart), echoing their D1 conjunction in the 7th — suggesting the same intensity that colors your partnership house also touches matters of courage and networks, in a milder, gains-oriented register (11th is a house of growth, not affliction).

4. D4 Caturthāṁśa — Property & Home

Your D4 Lagna is Libra, and here Jupiter — otherwise a planet requiring careful handling in your D1 (see Part 1, §5, §9) — lands directly in the Lagna itself. This is a genuinely encouraging, separate signal specifically for property and home matters: Jupiter's wisdom and expansive quality may express more freely and supportively here than in the domains it directly rules in D1 (siblings/communication, foreign/hidden matters). Venus, Mars, and Saturn cluster together in the 10th house of this chart, linking home/property themes to career and public standing in a structural way worth noting.

5. D7 Saptāṁśa — Children & Progeny

This is the single strongest divisional chart in your entire reading. Your D7 Lagna is Capricorn — the very same sign as your D1 Lagna, a Vargottama Lagna in its own right. Within this D7 Lagna sit Mars exalted, Saturn in its own sign, and Mercury — three planets, two of them in their best possible dignity, gathered in the house of self. Separately, Jupiter is exalted in the 7th house of this same chart.

Observation: D7 Lagna repeats the D1 Lagna sign exactly (Vargottama); Mars is exalted and Saturn is own-sign within that same Lagna house; Jupiter is exalted in the D7's 7th house.

Classical principle: The D7 (Saptāṁśa) is the dedicated chart for children and progeny; strength here — especially a Vargottama Lagna reinforced by exalted/own-sign occupants — is read as a genuinely favorable signature for this life domain.

Interpretation: Where your D1 shows Mars requiring real, earned strength (debilitated-but-cancelled) and Saturn carrying the weight of a demanding 7th-house conjunction, the D7 shows both of these same two planets in their most dignified possible form. This is a meaningful, favorable confirmation for matters of children specifically — the "hard-won strength" theme found elsewhere in your chart appears here already resolved into genuine dignity.

Prediction: This is offered as a generally favorable indicative signature for progeny-related matters, tempered by Moon's debilitation in this chart's 11th house (a single caution amid an otherwise strong picture) — best read together with the relevant daśā periods in Part 4.

6. D9 Navāṁśa — The Deeper Confirmation Layer

Applying the full D1+D9 comparison to every graha (see Part 1 for the raw D9 table), your chart shows an unusually harmonious relationship between the two charts: five of your seven classical grahas (Moon, Mercury, Venus, Saturn — plus Jupiter's mild improvement) show either a stable or improving D9 confirmation of their D1 promise. Only one placement — Mars, your Dārakāraka — shows a genuine, repeating weakness across both charts (debilitated in both D1 and D9), which is why this specific placement receives the most direct, honest attention throughout this reading rather than being softened.

7. D10 Daśāṁśa — Career & Public Role

Your D10 Lagna is Gemini. Unlike several of your other divisional charts, this one is comparatively muted in raw sign-dignity — no graha here is exalted or own-sign, and Saturn is debilitated in the 11th house of gains and networks. The one genuinely strong, direct signal is that Mars sits precisely in the 10th house of this D10 chart itself — a direct occupation of the career house within the career-dedicated divisional chart, even at ordinary dignity.

Observation: Mars occupies H10 of the D10 chart directly; Saturn is debilitated in the D10's 11th house (gains).

Classical principle: A planet's direct occupation of a house within its own dedicated divisional chart is a meaningful signal even without exalted dignity — presence and activation matter, not only strength.

Interpretation: This suggests your career is likely to be characterized more by direct action, initiative, and hands-on engagement (Mars's natural signification) than by smooth, effortless recognition. Saturn's debilitation in the gains house suggests that networking or peer/institutional recognition specifically may require more sustained, patient effort than the drive and capability itself.

Prediction: This aligns with, and reinforces, the D1's own career signature — Rāhu alone in the 10th house, and yogakāraka Venus ruling it — suggesting an ambitious, initiative-driven, possibly unconventional career path where the effort itself (Mars) may need to be sustained more than the recognition (Saturn) arrives automatically. Timing specifics are covered in Part 4.
