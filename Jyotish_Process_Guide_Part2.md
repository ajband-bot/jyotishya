## STEP 8 — Assess Planetary Dignity (Graha Avastha)

> *"Grahāṇāṃ ucca-nīca-sva-kṣetra-mitra-śatrādi-avastāḥ —  
> Phala-hāni-phala-vṛddhi-kārakāḥ sūryavat."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.3, Graha Guna Karma Adhyaya**  
> (A planet's dignity — exalted/debilitated/own sign/friendly/enemy — increases or decreases its results like the Sun amplifies or dims light)

### 8.1 The Five Primary Dignity States

| State | Sanskrit | Description | Strength |
|-------|----------|-------------|----------|

| **Own Sign** | Sva-kṣetra | Planet in its own ruled sign | 75% |

| **Enemy sign** | śatru-kṣetra | Planet in sign of a natural enemy | 12.5% |

### 8.5 Neechabhanga Raja Yoga (Debilitation Cancellation)

When a planet is debilitated, check for **Neechabhanga** (cancellation of debilitation).

> *"Nīca-grāme grāma-yoge nīca-bhaṅgastu rāja-yogaḥ;  
> Lagne vā kendre vā ucca-graho drṣṭā, nīca-bhaṅga iti smrţam."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.36** (Cancellation of debility becomes a Raja Yoga)

**Cancellation conditions (any one is sufficient)**:
1. The planet that would exalt in the debilitated planet's sign is in a Kendra from Lagna or Moon
2. The dispositor (sign lord) of the debilitated planet is in a Kendra from Lagna or Moon
3. The debilitated planet itself is in a Kendra or Trikona
4. The debilitated planet is exalted in the Navamsha (D9)
5. A natural benefic conjuncts or fully aspects the debilitated planet

## STEP 9 — Map Planetary Relationships

### 9.1 Naisargika Maitri (Natural Permanent Relationships)

> *"Naisargika-maitriḥ — janma-kāla-nirpekṣam, sadā eva."*  
> — BPHS Ch.3 (Natural friendships are birth-independent; they are permanent)

| Planet | Natural Friends | Neutral | Natural Enemies |
|--------|----------------|---------|----------------|
| **Sun** | Moon, Mars, Jupiter | Mercury | Venus, Saturn |
| **Moon** | Sun, Mercury | Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn | — |
| **Mars** | Sun, Moon, Jupiter | Venus, Saturn | Mercury |
| **Mercury** | Sun, Venus | Mars, Jupiter, Saturn | Moon |
| **Jupiter** | Sun, Moon, Mars | Saturn | Mercury, Venus |
| **Venus** | Mercury, Saturn | Mars, Jupiter | Sun, Moon |
| **Saturn** | Mercury, Venus | Jupiter | Sun, Moon, Mars |

### 9.2 Tatkalika Maitri (Temporary Chart-Specific Relationships)

In addition to permanent relationships, each planet forms **temporary relationships** based on its position in the birth chart.

> *"Tatkālika-mitratā — jannādhipo rāśyantara-gānām."*  
> — BPHS Ch.3

```
RULE:
  Count the signs occupied by a planet AND the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th,
  6th, and 12th signs FROM it. Planets in these 7 signs are
  TEMPORARY FRIENDS.
  Planets in the remaining 5 signs (7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th from it)
  are TEMPORARY ENEMIES.
```

### 9.3 Panchadha Maitri (Five-Fold Combined Relationship)

Combine Natural + Temporary to get the composite relationship:

| Natural | Temporary | Combined Result |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Friend | Friend | **Adhimitra (Best Friend)** |
| Friend | Neutral | **Friend** |
| Friend | Enemy | **Neutral** |
| Neutral | Friend | **Friend** |
| Neutral | Neutral | **Neutral** |
| Neutral | Enemy | **Enemy** |
| Enemy | Friend | **Neutral** |
| Enemy | Neutral | **Enemy** |
| Enemy | Enemy | **Adhishatru (Worst Enemy)** |

### 9.4 Why Relationships Matter

```
Applications:
  1. Dispositor chain: Planet in Sign → Sign Lord is Dispositor
     If Dispositor is friendly = planet well-supported
     If Dispositor is enemy    = planet undermined

  2. Yoga formation: Mutual friends in Kendra/Trikona = strong Raja Yoga

  3. Ashtakuta compatibility (Graha Maitri Kuta): Moon sign lords'
     relationship determines marital mental compatibility

  4. Transit (Gochara) support: Transiting planet in friendly sign
     gives better results
```

  Natural relationships of Mars: Friends = Sun, Moon, Jupiter
  Sun is in H2 (Sagittarius) — Jupiter's sign → Jupiter is Sun's friend

  → Jupiter is Mars' natural FRIEND and directly aspects Mars!

  Jupiter is in Pisces → Pisces lord = Jupiter (self-disposited!)

  → Very strong and pure Jupiterian expression ✔️

Mercury (H2, Sagittarius) dispositor:
  Sagittarius lord = Jupiter
  Mercury-Jupiter = NATURAL ENEMIES
  Jupiter is in H5 (a Trikona, strong position) → even hostile dispositor

## STEP 10 — Map Planetary Aspects (Graha Drishti)

> *"Saptamaṃ sarve grahāḥ paśyanti; Maṅgalaḥ caturthaṃ aṣṭamaṃ ca;  
> Guruḥ pañcamaṃ navamaṃ ca; Śan ir trţīyaṃ daśamaṃ ca."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.26, Graha Drıṣṭi Adhyaya**

**Translation**: All planets aspect the 7th house from themselves. Mars additionally aspects the 4th and 8th. Jupiter additionally aspects the 5th and 9th. Saturn additionally aspects the 3rd and 10th.

|--------|-----------------|-----------------|------------------|

| Ketu | Disputed; use Jataka Parijata (7th) | — | — |

> **Rule**: In Whole Sign aspects, if Mars is in H1, it aspects H4, H7, and H8 (by whole sign count).

### 10.2 Aspect Interpretation

```
Strength of aspect:
  Conjunction (same sign)  = Strongest influence (for good or bad)
  7th aspect              = Full aspect (100%)
  Special aspects (Mars/Jupiter/Saturn) = Full aspect (100%)

Nature of aspect:
  Benefic planet aspect   = Protection, grace, growth
  Malefic planet aspect   = Challenge, pressure, harshness
  Own-sign planet aspect  = Particularly powerful (for that planet)
```

### 10.4 The Rashi Drishti System (Alternative)

Bhava Drishti (whole-sign aspect per BPHS) is standard for Parashari work.  
Some authorities also use **Rashi Drishti** (sign-to-sign mutual aspects):

> *"Sthira-rāśyāḥ cara-rāśim paśyanti; cara-rāśyāḥ sthira-rāśim."*  

```
Fixed signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius)    → aspect all Movable signs EXCEPT adjacent
Movable signs (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn) → aspect all Fixed signs EXCEPT adjacent
Dual signs (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) → aspect all other Dual signs
```

This is used in Jaimini system and some advanced Parashari work, not standard for beginners.

## STEP 11 — House Lordship Analysis

### 11.1 Functional Benefics & Malefics (Lagna-Specific)

In Parashari Jyotish, a planet's benefic/malefic quality is determined by WHICH HOUSES it rules for a given Lagna — not just its natural quality.

> *"Lagnādhipo balavin mūrtimān — śubho yasya kāryasya kārakaḥ."*  
> — BPHS Ch.34, Yoga Adhyaya

**General Rule**:
- Lords of **Trikona** (H1, H5, H9) → **Natural Functional Benefics** for that Lagna
- Lords of **Kendra** (H1, H4, H7, H10) → Benefic if also Trikona lord; neutral otherwise
- Lords of **Dusthana** (H6, H8, H12) → **Functional Malefics** for that Lagna
- Lord of **H3, H11** → Mild functional malefics

### 11.2 Kendradhipati Dosha

> *"Kendradhipatī śublāḥ pāpimānah; pāpāḥ śulbhimānah."*  
> — BPHS Ch.34 (Natural benefics as Kendra lords lose beneficence; malefics as Kendra lords gain beneficence)

If a **natural benefic** (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Moon) lords ONLY a Kendra (not a Trikona), it suffers Kendradhipati Dosha and becomes functionally neutral/mildly malefic.

### 11.3 Worked Example — Scorpio Lagna House Lordships

```
H1  = Scorpio    → Lord: Mars    [Lagna lord = always benefic]
H2  = Sagittarius→ Lord: Jupiter [H2 = Maraka, but Jupiter also owns H5]
H3  = Capricorn  → Lord: Saturn  [H3 lord = mild malefic]
H4  = Aquarius   → Lord: Saturn  [H4 lord = Saturn owns H3+H4, Kendra only]
H5  = Pisces     → Lord: Jupiter [TRIKONA lord = STRONG BENEFIC ✔️]
H6  = Aries      → Lord: Mars    [Dusthana lord]
H7  = Taurus     → Lord: Venus   [H7 lord = Maraka]
H8  = Gemini     → Lord: Mercury [Dusthana lord]
H9  = Cancer     → Lord: Moon    [TRIKONA lord = STRONG BENEFIC ✔️]
H10 = Leo        → Lord: Sun     [Kendra lord only]
H11 = Virgo      → Lord: Mercury [H11 lord = mild malefic]
H12 = Libra      → Lord: Venus   [Dusthana lord + H7 lord = double Maraka]

Key Assessment for Scorpio Lagna:
  MOST BENEFIC:  Jupiter (H2+H5 lord, with H5=Trikona overriding H2=Maraka)
                 Moon (H9 lord, pure Trikona)
  RAJA YOGA POTENTIAL: Mars (H1+H6 lord) + Jupiter (H2+H5 lord)
  MARAKA PLANETS: Venus (H7+H12), Mercury (H8+H11)
  NEUTRAL: Sun (H10 lord, Kendra only)
  CAUTIOUS: Saturn (H3+H4 lord, Kendradhipati Dosha)
```

A **Yoga Karaka** is a single planet that simultaneously lords both a Kendra AND a Trikona — making it the single most powerful planet for that Lagna.

## STEP 12 — Yoga Identification

> *"Yogas are combinations whose results supersede individual planetary placements.  
> One yoga can make or break an entire chart."*

### 12.1 The Yoga Identification Process

```
For each candidate yoga, verify ALL conditions:

  STEP A: Identify the yoga's required planet(s) or house lord(s)
  STEP B: Check sign and house placement meet conditions
  STEP C: Check dignity (exalted/own sign preferred for strength)
  STEP D: Check combustion (combust planets weaken yogas)
  STEP E: Check aspects on the yoga-forming planets
  STEP F: Verify the yoga is not cancelled by a counter-condition
  STEP G: Assign strength (strong/moderate/weak) based on above
  STEP H: Timing — the yoga gives full results in its Dasha/Antardasha
```

### 12.2 Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas

The five great-person yogas. Each requires one of five planets in its own sign OR exaltation sign, in a Kendra house (H1, H4, H7, or H10).

> *"Pañca-mahāpuruṣa-yoga — Rucaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, Saśaḥ;  
> Kendre ucce sva-kṣetre sthitair grahair yogāḥ ete prokṭāḥ."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.36, Pancha Mahapurusha Yoga Adhyaya**

| Yoga | Planet | Condition | Key Result |
|------|--------|-----------|------------|
| **Ruchaka** | Mars | Own/exalted in Kendra | Military/leadership, brave, famous |
| **Bhadra** | Mercury | Own/exalted in Kendra | Scholar, eloquent, analytical genius |
| **Hamsa** | Jupiter | Own/exalted in Kendra | Noble, wise, fortunate, guru-like |
| **Malavya** | Venus | Own/exalted in Kendra | Beautiful, artistic, wealthy, sensual |
| **Sasha** | Saturn | Own/exalted in Kendra | Disciplined, authoritative, long-lived |

### 12.3 Ruchaka Yoga — Worked Example (Ajay Kumar)

```
Condition 1: Mars in own sign OR exaltation sign
  Mars at 210.538° → Scorpio = OWN SIGN ✔️

Condition 2: Mars in a Kendra (H1, H4, H7, or H10)
  Mars in H1 (Lagna) = KENDRA ✔️

Condition 3: Mars not combust
  |210.538 − 255.013| = 44.47° > 17° → NOT COMBUST ✔️

Condition 4: Not cancelled by severe affliction
  No major affliction to Mars (Jupiter aspects it beneficially) ✔️

→ RUCHAKA YOGA CONFIRMED ✔️ — STRONG GRADE

Reference:
  "Maṅgale kendre sva-kṣetre vā ucce vā — Rucaka-yoga jāyate;
   Tatphalam — śaurya-sampanno, netā, sena-pati-samaḥ,
   Rāja-mānyo, dīrgha-jīvī, vikrama-tejasvin."

```

### 12.4 Raja Yoga

> *"Kendre vā trikoṇe vā kendresha-trikonesha-sambandhaḥ — Rāja-yogaḥ."*  
> — BPHS Ch.36 (Connection between lords of Kendra and Trikona houses = Raja Yoga)

**Formation criteria** (any one of):
1. Lord of a Kendra (H1/4/7/10) **conjoins** lord of a Trikona (H1/5/9)
2. Lord of a Kendra **mutually aspects** lord of a Trikona
3. Lord of a Kendra is **in** a Trikona house
4. Lord of a Trikona is **in** a Kendra house
5. Kendra lord and Trikona lord are in **mutual exchange** (Parivartana)

```
Worked Example — Ajay Kumar:

Jupiter is lord of H5 (Trikona) AND H2 (Maraka/neutral)
Jupiter is IN H5 = Trikona → Trikona lord in Trikona
Jupiter aspects H1 (Kendra) via 9th aspect
→ Trikona lord (Jupiter) connected to Kendra (H1) = RAJA YOGA ✔️

Moon is lord of H9 (Trikona), placed in H6 (Dusthana)
→ Trikona lord in Dusthana = VIPARITA RAJA YOGA potential
   (H9 lord in H6: not classic Viparita, but similar effect)

Sun is lord of H10 (Kendra), in H2 with Mercury (H8/H11 lord)
→ Kendra lord + Dusthana lord conjunction = complex; net moderate
```

### 12.5 Viparita Raja Yoga

> *"Ribā-stāna-ādhipānau — ribādye evā sthitau yadi;  
> Viparīta-rāja-yogaḥ — tatphalaṃ rāja-tulya-vaibhavaḥ."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.41** (When lords of H6, H8, H12 are in each other's houses — Viparita Raja Yoga; results equal to a king's glory)

**Condition**: Lord of H6, H8, or H12 must be **in** H6, H8, or H12 (in each other's houses, not their own).

```
For Ajay Kumar (Scorpio Lagna):

H8 lord = Mercury (in H2 — not H6/8/12, so NOT Viparita)
H12 lord = Venus (in H3 — not H6/8/12, so NOT Viparita)

Note: Pure Viparita Raja Yoga is NOT formed here, but the Ruchaka
Yoga (Mars in H1) effectively delivers similar protective results.
```

### 12.8 Yoga Cancellation Rules

> *"Dosha-yoga-bhaṅgam — śubha-drṣṭi, ucca-sthāna, balavat-lagnesha."*  
> — General principle from BPHS

**A yoga may be cancelled/weakened if**:
1. The yoga-forming planet is **combust**
2. The yoga-forming planet is **in an enemy sign** (debilitated)
3. A powerful malefic **conjuncts** the yoga-forming planets
4. The yoga-forming planet occupies a **Dusthana** (H6, H8, H12)
5. The yoga-forming planet's **Navamsha** is debilitated (D9 weakness)
6. The yoga operates during a **weak Dasha** (enemy-planet Dasha)

> **Expert Principle**: A yoga gives its results during the **Dasha/Antardasha** of the planets involved. A superb yoga that never gets triggered by Dasha may show only partial results.

## STEP 13 — Shadbala (Six-Fold Planetary Strength)

> *"Ḥaḍbalāni — sthāna-bala, dig-bala, kāla-bala, chesta-bala, naisargika-bala, drika-bala."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.27, Shadbala Adhyaya**

### 13.1 The Six Strength Components

| Component | What It Measures | Quick Calculation Method |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------------|
| **Sthana Bala** | Positional strength (sign dignity, house) | Own/exalt = high; enemy/debil = low |
| **Dig Bala** | Directional strength | Jupiter/Mercury strong in H1; Saturn H7; Mars/Sun H10; Moon/Venus H4 |
| **Kala Bala** | Temporal strength | Day births: Sun, Jupiter, Venus strong. Night: Moon, Mars, Saturn |
| **Chesta Bala** | Motional strength | Retrograde > slow > direct > fast |
| **Naisargika Bala** | Natural strength (permanent) | Sun > Moon > Venus > Jupiter > Mercury > Mars > Saturn |
| **Drik Bala** | Aspectual strength | Benefic aspects received = positive; Malefic = negative |

### 13.2 Simplified Shadbala Assessment

For practical interpretation (without full mathematical computation):

```
POWERFUL planet (high Shadbala):
  ✔️ In own sign or exaltation
  ✔️ In a Kendra or Trikona
  ✔️ Not combust
  ✔️ Not retrograde (unless exalted retrograde)
  ✔️ Receiving aspect from natural benefic
  ✔️ In friendly Navamsha (D9)

WEAK planet (low Shadbala):
  ❌ Combust (within Sun's orb)
  ❌ Debilitated (in enemy sign)
  ❌ In Dusthana (H6, H8, H12)
  ❌ Retrograde in debilitation
  ❌ Hemmed between malefics (Papakartari)
  ❌ Debilitated in D9 Navamsha
```

## Common Exceptions & Edge Cases in Steps 8–13

### ⚠️ Exception 1 — Retrograde + Debilitation

When a planet is debilitated AND retrograde, interpretations diverge:
- **Traditional BPHS view**: Retrograde planet effectively moves "backward" through the sign and behaves as if in the previous sign (often its exaltation sign) — so debilitated retrograde = effectively exalted
- **Modern view**: Retrograde planets in debilitation create inner tension and delayed but eventual results
- **Expert resolution**: Treat as partial cancellation of debilitation; watch D9 for final determination

### ⚠️ Exception 2 — Exchange of Signs (Parivartana Yoga)

When Planet A is in Planet B's sign AND Planet B is in Planet A's sign:

> *"Parasparāṃśake sthitāu dvāu grahāu — parivartana-yogaḥ;  
> Tatphalam — ubhayos ca phalaṃ parasparasya vaibhavam."*  
> — **BPHS Ch.28** (Parivartana Yoga — both planets exchange results; very powerful)

In Parivartana: Both planets are **treated as if in their own sign** for strength purposes. Their houses become interconnected — events of one house affect the other.

### ⚠️ Exception 3 — Multiple Lordship Conflict

When a planet lords both a benefic AND a malefic house:
- **General rule**: The more powerful house lordship dominates
- **BPHS principle**: Trikona lordship overrides Maraka/Dusthana lordship (for benefics)
- Example: Jupiter for Scorpio Lagna owns H2 (Maraka) + H5 (Trikona) → H5 Trikona lordship makes Jupiter the most benefic planet despite H2 Maraka ownership

### ⚠️ Exception 4 — Yoga with Combust Planet

> *"Astamite śublā api — yoga-phalam nā dadanti."*  
> — BPHS (Even benefics, when combust, don't deliver yoga results)

Combust yoga-forming planets give **delayed, reduced, or internally expressed** yoga results. The yoga exists but the person may not fully manifest it until the combustion phase passes (e.g., during favorable transits when Sun moves away).

### ⚠️ Exception 5 — Dispositor Chain (Atmakaraka Override)

In Jaimini Jyotish (advanced), the **Atmakaraka** (planet highest in degree regardless of sign) overrides standard Parashari lordship analysis. This is a separate system and should NOT be mixed with standard Parashari yoga analysis without clear demarcation.
