These charts are not merely maps of one lifetime — they are windows into the architecture of karma accumulated across many births. The Maharishi Parāśara devoted specific chapters to each of these vargas, recognizing that the gross physical world (D1) is but one thin layer of a far deeper multi-dimensional reality.

## 9. D60 — Ṣaṣṭyāṁśa Chart (Past Life Karma — The Supreme Varga)
*The most profound divisional chart: past life karmas, soul's accumulated essence*

**The 60 Shashtyamsha Lords (BPHS Ch.6)** are named divine and demonic archetypes — each 30 seconds of a sign carries a distinct soul-quality. For Arjuna's approximate positions:

- **Lagna (Pisces 02°52′):** Falls in approximately the 6th Shashtyamsha of Pisces → deity: *Indumukha* (benefic, associated with the Moon's grace) — the soul arrived in this life under the Moon's protective grace, suggesting past lives of devotional practice and lunar-deity worship.

- **Venus (Pisces 28°38′):** Falls near the 58th Shashtyamsha of Pisces → the planet of deepest soul-merit arrives in a late-cycle, completion-oriented Shashtyamsha. This confirms the multi-lifetime accumulation of Venusian/devotional karma.

- **Mars (Aries 05°30′):** Falls in approximately the 11th Shashtyamsha of Aries → *Ghora* (fierce) classification — this Shashtyamsha for Mars indicates that in past lives, Mars-energy was deployed in fierce, warrior-like contexts. The soul has warrior-karma that in this life is being transmuted into the warrior of dharma and creative enterprise.

- **Moon (Sagittarius 03°45′):** Falls in the 8th Shashtyamsha of Sagittarius — a spiritual, transitional Shashtyamsha. The Moon's past-life context involves spiritual seeking, pilgrimage, and philosophical inquiry across multiple lifetimes.

### D60 — The Overarching Past Life Reading:

The D60 synthesizes the deepest karmic themes across all lifetimes. For Arjuna Sharma, the overall D60 picture reveals:

**Theme 1 — The Devotee-Warrior:** The soul carries memories of lives as both a devotee (Lunar-Venusian karma, multiple devotional lifetimes) and a warrior or ruler (Martian Ghora Shashtyamsha, ancestral Kṣatriya energy). These two streams — spiritual surrender and active world-engagement — create the chart's fundamental creative tension. In this life, the integration of these two streams is the soul's central task.

**Theme 2 — The Searcher of Roots:** Moon in Mūla nakshatra at the deepest level (D1 + D27 confirmation) speaks of a soul that has repeatedly sought the root of truth across incarnations. This is a philosophical soul who, lifetime after lifetime, refuses to accept inherited answers — who digs deeper. The current life brings this seeking to its most productive expression.

**Theme 3 — The Incomplete Relationship:** Venus retrograde in D1, combined with Mercury and Venus as 7th and 8th lords in lagna — there is a past-life relationship story that arrives incomplete into this birth. A love, a partnership, a creative collaboration — something deeply significant was left unresolved. The soul returns carrying this unfinished thread, which is why relationships occupy such a central and karmic place in Arjuna's life.

**Theme 4 — The Suppressed Teacher:** Jupiter debilitated in D1 (with Neecha Bhanga) — in a prior life, the wisdom-teaching function (Jupiter) was suppressed, perhaps through authority, dogma, or external circumstance. The teacher could not teach; the guru could not impart. This life's Neecha Bhanga Rāja Yoga for Jupiter is the resolution: the teaching function that was suppressed is now returned, transformed through the very limitation that once blocked it. Arjuna will teach, guide, or impart wisdom — but through lived experience and hard-won understanding, not inherited doctrine.
