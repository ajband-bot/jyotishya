## 📜 Foreword — Why Vargas Matter

> *"Varge varge graha-phalam pṛthak-pṛthak vicintayet;*
> *Sarva-vargottamaḥ śreṣṭho, balavān eva sarvadā."*
> — **BPHS, Adhyāya 6, śloka 11**

Each varga is a *koṣa* (sheath) of a planet. The D1 shows the *body* of life-events; the vargas show the *organs*. A planet weak in D1 but strong across multiple vargas (vargottama, mūla-trikoṇa-by-varga, kendra-by-varga) recovers — even **rules** — its domain. Ajay's chart has *several* such recoveries, which is why the practical fruit of his life exceeds the surface arithmetic of D1 alone.

We use the **Ṣoḍaśa-varga** scheme (Parāśara). In this Part 2 we will read the six vargas most decisive for outer-life results. Part 3 covers the inner/karmic vargas.
